Full-arch dental implants in Houston

Full-arch dental implants in Houston replace a whole upper or lower set of teeth with a fixed bridge held by 4 to 6 implants. You may hear names like All-on-4. At Bayou City Implant Center, candidacy depends on your exam and scan, and results vary.

Who asks about full-arch teeth

  • Most of your teeth in one jaw are failing or gone
  • Your denture slips, rubs or makes eating hard
  • You were told many teeth need to come out
  • You want teeth that stay in, not ones you take out at night
  • You are healthy enough for oral surgery
  • You can keep up with cleanings and home care
  • You want to compare fixed teeth with a snap-in denture

How full-arch treatment works

  1. 1Consult and 3D CT scanThe dentist checks your bone and bite and walks you through fixed and removable options.

Questions about full-arch implant teeth

How much do full-arch implants cost in Houston?

Usually about $15,000 to $35,000 per arch, and zirconia can run higher. Ours starts at $19,500 per arch, and extractions or grafts can add to it.

Do I get teeth the same day?

Many patients leave with a temporary arch, but it depends on your bone and how the implants seat. The dentist will tell you after surgery.

Is full-arch better than a denture?

It stays fixed and does not cover the roof of your mouth, but it costs more. A snap-in denture is a lower cost option.

What are the risks?

Risks include infection, implant failure and nerve or sinus problems. The dentist explains them before you decide.
