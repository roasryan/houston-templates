Implant-supported dentures in Houston

Implant-supported dentures in Houston snap onto 2 to 4 implants so the denture stays in place when you eat and talk. You still take it out to clean it. At Bayou City Implant Center, candidacy depends on your exam and scan.

Who it can help

  • Your lower denture lifts or slides when you eat
  • You use a lot of denture glue
  • Sore spots keep coming back
  • You want a lower cost option than a fixed full arch
  • You are fine taking the denture out at night
  • You may be able to use your current denture
  • Your bone can hold at least 2 implants

How the process works

  1. 1Consult and 3D CT scanThe dentist checks your bone and your current denture.
  2. 2Implants placedUsually 2 to 4 implants go into the jaw.
  3. 3HealingAbout 3 to 6 months, and you wear a soft-lined denture meanwhile.
  4. 4Attachments addedSnaps go on the implants and into a new or adjusted denture.
  5. 5Fit and careYou learn to snap it in, take it out and clean around the implants.

Good to know in Houston

Houston has many longtime denture wearers, and a loose lower denture is the most common complaint we hear. Bring your current denture to the consult so the dentist can check it.

Dentures and implants are dental care regulated by the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners. Our dentist is a general dentist.

Questions about implant-supported dentures

How much are implant dentures in Houston?

Usually about $5,000 to $20,000 per arch, based on the number of implants. Ours starts at $8,500 per arch with two implants.

Can I use my current denture?

Sometimes. The dentist checks its fit and condition at the consult.

Do I take it out at night?

Yes. A snap-in denture comes out for cleaning and sleep.

How long do the snaps last?

The small inserts wear and are often replaced about once a year. It varies.
