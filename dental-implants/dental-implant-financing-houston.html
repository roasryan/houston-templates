Questions about financing and payment plans

Do you take CareCredit?

Yes, in this demo. Read the promo terms, since deferred interest can be charged back if the balance is not paid in time.

Will checking hurt my credit?

Many lenders let you check if you prequalify with a soft credit check. A full application may use a hard pull.

Can I pay in stages?

Yes. Many plans are paid as each step is done, such as the implant, then the crown.

Does the membership cover implants?

No. The Smile Membership is not insurance. It covers cleanings and gives 15% off most other care.
