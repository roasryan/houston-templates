Dental implant questions, answered

General

How much do dental implants cost in Houston?

One implant with an abutment and crown usually runs about $3,000 to $6,000 in Houston. A full arch is often $15,000 to $35,000 per jaw. These are typical ranges, not quotes, and your fee depends on your exam and scan.

Am I a candidate for implants?

It depends on your bone, gums and health. Smoking, uncontrolled diabetes and some medicines can change the plan. Your exam and 3D CT scan answer this.

Does insurance cover implants?

Some dental plans pay part, but yearly limits are often $1,000 to $2,000. We check your benefits and show what you would owe in writing.

Does it hurt?

The area is numbed, and many people feel pressure, not sharp pain. Some soreness for a few days is common. Sedation is an option, and the dentist explains the risks.

Is your dentist a specialist?

No. Our dentist is a general dentist who places implants. When a case needs an oral surgeon or periodontist, we refer you.

Single tooth implants

How much is a single tooth implant in Houston?

Usually about $3,000 to $6,000 for the implant, abutment and crown. Ours starts at $3,800, and grafts or extraction cost extra if you need them.

How long does it take?

Often 3 to 6 months from surgery to final crown, and longer if a graft is needed first.

How long do implants last?

Many last for many years with good care, but no outcome is guaranteed. Smoking and gum disease raise the risk of problems.

Can I get a tooth the same day?

Sometimes a temporary tooth is possible. It depends on your bone and your scan.

Full-arch implant teeth

How much do full-arch implants cost in Houston?

Usually about $15,000 to $35,000 per arch, and zirconia can run higher. Ours starts at $19,500 per arch, and extractions or grafts can add to it.

Do I get teeth the same day?

Many patients leave with a temporary arch, but it depends on your bone and how the implants seat. The dentist will tell you after surgery.

Is full-arch better than a denture?

It stays fixed and does not cover the roof of your mouth, but it costs more. A snap-in denture is a lower cost option.

What are the risks?

Risks include infection, implant failure and nerve or sinus problems. The dentist explains them before you decide.

Bone grafting and sinus lifts

How much does a bone graft cost in Houston?

A socket graft is usually about $250 to $1,200. Ridge grafts and sinus lifts cost more. Ours starts at $450 for a socket graft.

Does everyone need a graft?

No. Your 3D CT scan shows whether you have enough bone.

Does a bone graft hurt?

The area is numbed. Swelling and soreness for a few days are common.

How long before I can get the implant?

Often 3 to 6 months, but it depends on the graft and how you heal.

Implant-supported dentures

How much are implant dentures in Houston?

Usually about $5,000 to $20,000 per arch, based on the number of implants. Ours starts at $8,500 per arch with two implants.

Can I use my current denture?

Sometimes. The dentist checks its fit and condition at the consult.

Do I take it out at night?

Yes. A snap-in denture comes out for cleaning and sleep.

How long do the snaps last?

The small inserts wear and are often replaced about once a year. It varies.

Why do I need a 3D CT scan?

It shows bone height, width, nerves and sinuses, which a flat x-ray cannot show well.

Is the scan safe?

It uses a low dose of radiation. Tell us if you are pregnant.

Cost

Does insurance cover dental implants?

Some plans pay part, often up to a yearly limit of $1,000 to $2,000. Original Medicare does not cover routine dental care.

Why do implant prices differ so much?

Prices change with grafts, materials, sedation and whether the crown is included. Compare full written plans.

Are cheap implant ads real?
