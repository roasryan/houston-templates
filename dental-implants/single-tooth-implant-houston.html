Single tooth implants in Houston

A single tooth implant in Houston replaces one missing tooth with a small post in the jaw, an abutment and a crown. Bayou City Implant Center starts every case with a 3D CT scan and an exam. Candidacy depends on your exam and scan, and results vary.

When a single implant may fit

  • You lost one tooth to decay, a crack or an injury
  • A tooth is set to be pulled and you want a plan first
  • You do not want to grind down healthy teeth for a bridge
  • A gap makes chewing on one side hard
  • Your gums are healthy, or can be treated first
  • You have enough bone, or a graft can add it
  • You are ready for a few months of healing

How a single implant is done

  1. 1Consult and 3D CT scanThe dentist checks your bone, gums and bite, and gives you a written plan with fees.
  2. 2Extraction or graft, if neededA damaged tooth comes out, and bone may be added to the socket.
  3. 3Implant placedThe post goes into the jaw with numbing, and sedation if you choose it.
  4. 4HealingThe bone grows around the post over about 3 to 6 months. You come in for healing checks.
  5. 5Crown placedAn abutment and a custom crown are attached and your bite is checked.

Good to know in Houston

Houston summers are hot, so plan cool, soft foods and plenty of water for the first days after surgery. If you have sedation, bring a driver, since the drive home on 59 or 610 is not one to do groggy.

Dentists in Texas are licensed by the Texas State Board of Dental Examiners. Our dentist is a general dentist, and sedation is only given under a board-issued anesthesia permit.

Questions about single tooth implants

How much is a single tooth implant in Houston?

Usually about $3,000 to $6,000 for the implant, abutment and crown. Ours starts at $3,800, and grafts or extraction cost extra if you need them.

How long does it take?

Often 3 to 6 months from surgery to final crown, and longer if a graft is needed first.

How long do implants last?

Many last for many years with good care, but no outcome is guaranteed. Smoking and gum disease raise the risk of problems.

Can I get a tooth the same day?

Sometimes a temporary tooth is possible. It depends on your bone and your scan.
