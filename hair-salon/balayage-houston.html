Balayage in Houston, TX

Balayage in Houston is a hand-painted color that grows out softly, so you can go longer between visits. At Magnolia & Main balayage starts at $310 and partial highlights at $195, and your price is confirmed at a free color consult.

Is balayage or highlights right

  • You want a sun-kissed look, not stripes
  • You want fewer salon visits a year
  • You are going lighter in steps, not all at once
  • You like soft, blended roots
  • You have dark hair and want brighter ends
  • You want to add dimension to blonde
  • You are ready to spend 3 to 4 hours in the chair

Good to know in Houston

Houston sun and pool water can turn blonde brassy. A purple shampoo once a week and a leave-in with UV protection help keep balayage fresh. Hard water also dulls it, so a shower filter can be worth a look.

Stylists must hold a TDLR cosmetology license. Lightener is a strong chemical, so a strand test and a stylist who knows your hair history matter.

Questions about highlights and balayage

How much does balayage cost in Houston?

It usually runs about $200 to $450, depending on hair length and stylist level. Ours starts at $310.

How long does balayage take?

Usually 3 to 4 hours.

How long does balayage last?

The soft root means 3 to 4 months between visits for many people. A gloss in between keeps the tone.

Will balayage damage my hair?

Lightener can dry hair. A stylist can lower the risk with bond care and a plan that fits your hair.
