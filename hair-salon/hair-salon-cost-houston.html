What changes the price

  • Stylist level and demand.
  • Hair length and thickness.
  • How many steps your color takes.
  • Product used: bond builders, toners and smoothing kits cost more.
  • Time in the chair: color is often priced by the hour.
  • Neighborhood: Heights, River Oaks and Uptown tend to cost more than the suburbs.
  • Add-ons like a cut after color.

Ways to save

  • Book a free color consult before you commit to a color.
  • Pick a lower-maintenance color like balayage with a soft root.
  • Use a membership: Blowout Club is $99 a month for two blowouts.
  • Ask if a junior stylist is available, usually at a lower price.
  • Use color-safe products at home so your color lasts longer.

Cost questions

Should I tip on top of the price?

Most people tip 15 to 20 percent. It is not included in our prices.

Do you charge for cancellations?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for no charge. Later cancels and no-shows are charged 50% of the service.

Is a deposit required?

A deposit holds color appointments and goes toward your service.

Why are prices a range?

Hair length, thickness and the work needed all change the time and product. A consult gives you the exact number.
