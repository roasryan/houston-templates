Hair salon questions, answered

General

How much does balayage cost in Houston?

Balayage in Houston usually runs about $200 to $450 depending on hair length and stylist level. Ours starts at $310, and you get the exact price at the free color consult.

How long does color take?

A root touch-up takes about 90 minutes. All-over color takes about 2 hours, and balayage can take 3 to 4 hours.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for no charge. Later cancels and no-shows are charged 50% of the service.

Can I bring a photo?

Yes, please do. A photo helps your stylist see what you want. We’ll tell you honestly what is possible in one visit.

Are your stylists licensed?

Yes. Texas requires a TDLR cosmetology license for stylists and a licensed salon. Licenses and the complaint notice are posted at the front desk.

Cuts and styling

How much is a haircut in Houston?

Women’s cuts in Houston usually run about $50 to $120 and men’s about $30 to $65. Ours start at $75 and $45.

How often should I get a haircut?

Most people go every 6 to 10 weeks. Shorter or layered cuts do better every 6 to 8 weeks.

Do I need to wash my hair before I come?

No. Your stylist washes it. Just come with it clean enough to style.

Can I bring a photo?

Yes. A photo helps. Your stylist will tell you what will work with your hair.

Blowouts

How much is a blowout in Houston?

Usually $40 to $85. Ours is $55.

How long does a blowout last?

Usually 2 to 4 days, depending on your hair and the weather.

Can I get a blowout on dirty hair?

Your stylist washes your hair as part of the service, so come as you are.

Is a blowout good for curly hair?

Yes. A stylist can stretch curls into a smooth look or shape them into soft waves.

Color and root touch-ups

How much is hair color in Houston?

Root touch-ups usually run $70 to $150 and all-over color $90 to $220. Ours start at $110 and $150.

How often should I color my roots?

Most people go every 4 to 6 weeks.

Can I go from dark to light in one visit?

Sometimes. It depends on your hair history and health. Your stylist will tell you honestly at the consult.

Do I need a patch test?

If it is your first time with us or with a new color, yes. It is a quick test on the skin.

Highlights and balayage

How much does balayage cost in Houston?

It usually runs about $200 to $450, depending on hair length and stylist level. Ours starts at $310.

How long does balayage take?

Usually 3 to 4 hours.

How long does balayage last?

The soft root means 3 to 4 months between visits for many people. A gloss in between keeps the tone.

Will balayage damage my hair?

Lightener can dry hair. A stylist can lower the risk with bond care and a plan that fits your hair.

Silk press and smoothing

How much is a silk press in Houston?

It usually runs about $80 to $180. Ours is $110.

How long does a silk press last?

About one to two weeks. Humidity and sweat shorten it.

Does a silk press damage hair?

Heat can dry hair if it is done often or at high temperatures. A careful stylist uses a protectant and checks your hair first.

Is keratin safe?

Some keratin products release fumes. Ask what the product contains and that the room is ventilated.

Bridal trials

How much is a bridal hair trial in Houston?

Usually $100 to $250. Ours is $175.

When should I book my trial?

About 4 to 8 weeks before the wedding.

Can I bring my veil?

Yes. Bring anything that will be in your hair on the day.

Is a trial worth it?

It lets you see the style and time before the wedding day, so there are fewer surprises.

Cost

Should I tip on top of the price?

Most people tip 15 to 20 percent. It is not included in our prices.

Do you charge for cancellations?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for no charge. Later cancels and no-shows are charged 50% of the service.

Is a deposit required?

A deposit holds color appointments and goes toward your service.

Why are prices a range?

Hair length, thickness and the work needed all change the time and product. A consult gives you the exact number.
