Hair color in Houston, TX

Hair color in Houston works best when you plan it with a stylist first. At Magnolia & Main, root touch-ups start at $110 and all-over color at $150, with a patch test the first time. A free color consult gets you a plan and a price.

Good to know before color

  • Bring photos of the shade you like and the shade you hate
  • Tell your stylist about past box color, henna or bleach
  • Gray coverage and highlights are different jobs
  • Expect 90 minutes for a root touch-up and about 2 hours for all-over color
  • A patch test is done before new color to check for a reaction
  • Color needs a refresh every 4 to 6 weeks at the roots
  • Color-safe shampoo and cool water help it last

How a color visit works

  1. 1Free consultYour stylist checks your hair and scalp, and talks about the color, time and price.
  2. 2Patch testA small test on the skin before new color if it is your first time.
  3. 3Mix and applyYour color is mixed for your hair and applied to the roots or all over.
  4. 4ProcessColor sits for the time needed while you relax.
  5. 5Rinse, gloss and styleA rinse, conditioner and a blow-dry so you see the final shade.

Good to know in Houston

Sun and chlorine in Houston fade color quickly, and hard water leaves buildup that dulls it. A color-safe shampoo and an occasional clarifying wash help. If you swim, wet your hair with fresh water first.

Stylists need a TDLR cosmetology license, and a patch test is the standard way to check for a color allergy.

Questions about color and root touch-ups

How much is hair color in Houston?

Root touch-ups usually run $70 to $150 and all-over color $90 to $220. Ours start at $110 and $150.

How often should I color my roots?

Most people go every 4 to 6 weeks.

Can I go from dark to light in one visit?

Sometimes. It depends on your hair history and health. Your stylist will tell you honestly at the consult.

Do I need a patch test?

If it is your first time with us or with a new color, yes. It is a quick test on the skin.
