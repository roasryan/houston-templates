Silk press in Houston, TX

A silk press in Houston straightens natural or textured hair with heat and no chemical relaxer. At Magnolia & Main it is $110. Keratin smoothing treatments start at $375 and are done in a well-ventilated room.

Silk press vs. keratin smoothing

  • Silk press is heat only, and it washes out with moisture
  • Keratin smoothing uses a product and lasts longer, usually a few months
  • Silk press is good for a special event or a change
  • Keratin can cut daily styling time in humid weather
  • Both need healthy hair to look best
  • Tell your stylist about past relaxers or color
  • Ask about product safety and ventilation

Good to know in Houston

Humidity is the enemy of a silk press in Houston. Wrap your hair at night, skip heat styling for a week, and keep a small can of anti-humidity spray handy. A good press can last one to two weeks.

Keratin and smoothing products can release formaldehyde fumes. OSHA’s formaldehyde standard applies to salons, which is why we use a ventilated room and keep safety sheets on hand.

Questions about silk press and smoothing

How much is a silk press in Houston?

It usually runs about $80 to $180. Ours is $110.

How long does a silk press last?

About one to two weeks. Humidity and sweat shorten it.

Does a silk press damage hair?

Heat can dry hair if it is done often or at high temperatures. A careful stylist uses a protectant and checks your hair first.

Is keratin safe?

Some keratin products release fumes. Ask what the product contains and that the room is ventilated.
