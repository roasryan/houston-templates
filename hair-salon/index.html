Questions

How much does balayage cost in Houston?

Balayage in Houston usually runs about $200 to $450 depending on hair length and stylist level. Ours starts at $310, and you get the exact price at the free color consult.

How long does color take?

A root touch-up takes about 90 minutes. All-over color takes about 2 hours, and balayage can take 3 to 4 hours.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for no charge. Later cancels and no-shows are charged 50% of the service.

Can I bring a photo?

Yes, please do. A photo helps your stylist see what you want. We’ll tell you honestly what is possible in one visit.

Are your stylists licensed?

Yes. Texas requires a TDLR cosmetology license for stylists and a licensed salon. Licenses and the complaint notice are posted at the front desk.
