Bridal hair trial in Houston, TX

A bridal hair trial in Houston is a practice run for your wedding day look. At Magnolia & Main it is $175, and you leave with a plan, photos and a clear time for the big day.

How to get ready for a trial

  • Book 4 to 8 weeks before the wedding
  • Bring photos of the hairstyle and the dress neckline
  • Bring your veil, comb or hair accessories
  • Wear a top similar to your dress neckline
  • Wash your hair the day before if you want natural grip
  • Plan color and cut at least 2 weeks before the trial
  • Take photos from the back, side and front

What happens at the trial

  1. 1TalkYour stylist asks about the venue, the dress, the time and the weather.
  2. 2Try a styleAn updo, half-up or waves, built and pinned.
  3. 3AdjustYou change the height, the pull and the shape until it feels right.
  4. 4Photos and notesYour stylist photographs the look and writes the plan.
  5. 5Day-of planYou agree on timing for you and your party on the wedding day.

Good to know in Houston

Many Houston weddings are outdoors or in a warm ballroom, and humidity can wreck a loose style. Ask your stylist for a style with good hold and a humidity-proof prep. Spring and fall events often bring a breeze, so plan for that too.

Questions about bridal trials

How much is a bridal hair trial in Houston?

Usually $100 to $250. Ours is $175.

When should I book my trial?

About 4 to 8 weeks before the wedding.

Can I bring my veil?

Yes. Bring anything that will be in your hair on the day.

Is a trial worth it?

It lets you see the style and time before the wedding day, so there are fewer surprises.
