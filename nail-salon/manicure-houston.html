Manicure in Houston, TX

A manicure in Houston is a simple way to reset your hands. At Lotus Bay Nail Lounge, the classic manicure is $30 and includes shaping, cuticle care and polish. It takes about 30 minutes.

Good to know before your manicure

  • A classic manicure uses regular polish, which dries in minutes
  • Regular polish lasts about a week
  • Tell your tech if you bite your nails or have hangnails
  • Bring your own color if you have a favorite
  • Skip shaving or waxing your hands first
  • Tell your tech about any cuts or skin conditions
  • Give polish time to dry before you reach for your keys

What happens at your manicure

  1. 1Choose a shapeSquare, round, oval or almond. Your tech files to match.
  2. 2Soak and cuticle careHands soak briefly, then cuticles are pushed back and trimmed.
  3. 3Buff and cleanNails are lightly buffed and wiped clean.
  4. 4PolishBase coat, two coats of color and a top coat.
  5. 5Lotion and dryA short hand massage with lotion while the polish dries.

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s heat makes hands sweat and polish can smear on a humid day, so allow extra dry time before you drive. A quick-dry top coat and cuticle oil are worth the small extra.

Manicurists must hold a TDLR license, and files and buffers that touch skin are single-use.
