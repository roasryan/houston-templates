What nail services cost in Houston

Nail salon prices in Houston usually run from about $20 for a plain manicure to $100 or more for a full set with art. These are typical ranges, not quotes. Where we list our own prices, they belong to this demo salon.

What changes the price

  • Type of service and the polish used.
  • Nail length and shape.
  • Nail art, chrome and charms.
  • Removal of old gel or acrylic.
  • Group size or a private room.
  • Salon level and neighborhood.

Ways to save

  • Join a plan: Gel Club is $39 a month for one gel manicure.
  • Keep your set maintained so you do not need a rebuild.
  • Ask for the price of art or chrome before you start.
  • Book a weekday slot, which is easier to get.
  • Choose a simple color with your set and add art later.

Cost questions

Should I tip my nail tech?

Most people tip 15 to 20 percent.

What is your cancel policy?

Cancel at least 12 hours ahead for no charge. No-shows are charged $20.

Is soak-off bad for nails?

Soak-off is safer than peeling. Your tech can do it.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, but booking holds your time, especially on weekends.
