Acrylic, Gel-X or dip

  • Acrylic is strong and good for long nails
  • Gel-X uses soft gel tips and feels lighter
  • Dip powder is a good middle option for short to medium nails
  • Acrylic and Gel-X need a fill every 2 to 4 weeks
  • Do not pick or peel them off at home
  • Plan a soak-off for removal

Good to know in Houston

Houston heat and humidity can make nails lift if they get wet too often. Wear gloves when you wash dishes and keep your hands dry, and book your fill before the gap grows.

Manicurists need a TDLR license. Texas bans MMA liquid in nail products, and our products come with safety sheets and ventilation.
