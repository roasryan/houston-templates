Nail salon questions, answered

General

How much is a gel manicure in Houston?

Most Houston salons charge about $35 to $55 for a gel manicure. Ours is $42, and Gel-X sets start at $75.

How long do gel nails last?

Gel polish usually lasts 2 to 3 weeks. Acrylic and Gel-X sets last 3 to 4 weeks and need a fill after that.

How do I know the tools are clean?

Texas rules require tools to be cleaned and disinfected after each client, and pedicure tubs to be cleaned after every client. Files and buffers that touch skin are used once.

Do you do waxing?

We offer eyebrow waxing at $12. Waxing in Texas takes an esthetician or cosmetology license, and our wax room is staffed by licensed staff.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 12 hours ahead and there’s no charge. No-shows are charged $20.

Gel manicure

How much is a gel manicure in Houston?

Most Houston salons charge about $35 to $55. Ours is $42.

How long does gel last?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks.

Is gel bad for my nails?

Gel itself is not harmful when applied and removed properly. Peeling it off yourself can damage nails, so soak it off.

Can I get gel on short nails?

Yes. Gel looks good on short natural nails.

How long does regular polish last?

About a week, depending on how hard you use your hands.

Do you do men’s manicures?

Spa pedicures

How much is a pedicure in Houston?

A classic pedicure usually runs $30 to $55. Ours is $40.

Are pedicures safe?

They are when the salon follows Texas cleaning rules. Look for a fresh liner and clean tools.

How often should I get a pedicure?

Every 3 to 4 weeks for most people.

Can I get a pedicure with a cut or athlete’s foot?

Tell your tech first. Some conditions should be treated by a doctor before a pedicure.

Acrylics, Gel-X and dip

How much do acrylic nails cost in Houston?

Full sets usually run about $40 to $85. Ours start at $55.

How long do acrylics last?

3 to 4 weeks before a fill.

Which is better, acrylic or Gel-X?
