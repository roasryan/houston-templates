Brake repair in Houston, TX

Brake repair in Houston is mostly about stop-and-go traffic, heat and heavy trucks around you. We measure your pads and rotors, show you photos, and send the price in writing. Nothing gets fixed until you say yes.

Signs you need brake repair

  • A squeal or high whine when you press the pedal
  • A grinding or scraping sound, which often means metal on metal
  • The pedal feels soft, low or spongy
  • The car pulls to one side when you brake
  • The steering wheel shakes when you slow down from highway speed
  • The brake light on the dash is on
  • Brake dust is thick on one wheel
  • The car takes longer to stop than it used to

What changes the price

  • Car size and make. European cars and trucks cost more.
  • Pad type. Ceramic pads cost more and run quieter with less dust.
  • Whether the rotors can stay or must be replaced.
  • Brake fluid, calipers or hoses that also need work.
  • Labor rate and shop supplies. Ours is $149 an hour for general repair.
  • OE dealer parts versus quality aftermarket parts.

Good to know in Houston

Houston traffic means lots of hard braking on I-610, I-45 and Highway 290. Heat and humidity age brake fluid faster, and heavy rain can rust rotors that sit for days. Short trips in town wear pads sooner than steady highway miles.

Brake work does not need a permit in Texas. Shops should give you a written estimate, and you can ask for your old parts back.

Questions about brake repair

How long do brake pads last in Houston?

Usually 30,000 to 70,000 miles. City driving and towing wear them faster. We measure them so you know how much is left.

Can I drive with grinding brakes?

It is risky. Grinding usually means the pads are gone and the rotors are being cut up. Get it checked soon.

Do I need new rotors with new pads?

Not always. If the rotors measure above minimum thickness and are smooth, they can stay. We show you the numbers.

Is the front brake inspection really free?

Yes. We look, measure and send photos. You pay only if you approve a repair.
