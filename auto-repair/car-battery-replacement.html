Car battery and starter help in Houston

Car battery replacement in Houston is common because summer heat wears batteries out in about three years. We test the battery, starter and alternator first so you only replace what failed. Many fixes are done the same day if we have the part.

Signs you need a battery or starter

  • The engine cranks slowly
  • You hear a click and nothing happens
  • The headlights look dim at idle
  • The battery or charging light is on
  • The battery is more than three years old
  • Corrosion on the battery posts
  • The car needs jumps more than once
  • Electronics reset or flicker

Good to know in Houston

Houston heat is the number one killer of batteries because heat speeds up internal wear. Batteries here often fail in summer, but you feel it in the first cool morning. Short trips in traffic also keep batteries from fully charging.

Old batteries contain lead and acid and must be recycled. We take care of this, and Texas requires retailers to take back old batteries.

Questions about battery and starting

How long does a car battery last in Houston?

Usually 3 to 5 years. Heat shortens that, so test it yearly after year three.

How do I know it is the battery and not the alternator?

If the car starts with a jump but dies soon after, the alternator is a suspect. We test both.

Can you replace it the same day?

Often yes. Common batteries are quick to swap. Special batteries may need to be ordered.

Do you recycle the old battery?

Yes. It is recycled as required.
