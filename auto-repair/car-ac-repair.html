Car A/C repair in Houston, TX

Car A/C repair in Houston matters from April to October. We find why your A/C is blowing warm air instead of just adding refrigerant. A certified tech tests for leaks, shows you what failed and sends the price in writing.

Signs your car A/C needs repair

  • Air is warm or only slightly cool
  • The A/C is cold on the highway but warm at red lights
  • A weak flow of air from the vents
  • A musty smell when you turn it on
  • A clicking or rattling noise from the engine bay
  • Water pooling inside the car, usually on the passenger floor
  • The A/C works, then quits after a few minutes
  • Wet, oily spots on the A/C lines

  • Which part failed.

  • Whether the dash must come apart, as for an evaporator.
  • Car make, since European cars cost more.

Good to know in Houston

Houston runs the A/C more months than most of the country, and the humidity makes weak cooling feel worse. A slow leak that costs little in March can leave you hot in July, so repair it before summer. Parked cars in direct sun also age rubber seals and hoses.

Anyone who handles car refrigerant must hold EPA Section 609 certification, and the refrigerant must be recovered, not vented. Our A/C techs hold it.

Questions about a/c repair

Why is my A/C blowing warm air?

Most often a small leak has let the refrigerant out. We find the leak first instead of just adding more.

How much does a car A/C recharge cost in Houston?

Usually $150 to $350, more for newer cars with R-1234yf. It depends on the leak and the car.

Is an A/C recharge a permanent fix?

Only if nothing leaks. If refrigerant escaped, it will again, so we test for leaks.

How long does an A/C repair take?

A check takes about an hour. Many repairs are same day. Compressor and evaporator jobs can take longer.
