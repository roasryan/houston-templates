Pest control in Alvin, TX

Pest control in Alvin, Texas, mixes in-town homes with rural lots and farmland. The city south of Houston sits along Highway 35 and Highway 6, in zip code 77511, near Mustang Bayou and rice and farm fields. Homes range from older houses near downtown to newer subdivisions and acreage properties.

Open fields, drainage ditches and wet soil bring mosquitoes, fire ants and rodents close to homes. Rats and mice move from fields into barns, garages and attics, especially after harvest or heavy rain. Larger lots mean more area to treat and more places for pests to hide.

Termites are a long-term worry in the coastal climate. Wood fences, sheds and decks that touch soil are easy targets. We inspect the structure and any wood near the ground, and we give a written estimate before treatment.

Most Alvin customers start with a quarterly or bi-monthly plan, add fire ant control for the yard and mosquito service in warm months. Rodent control includes sealing entry points, not just trapping. Barns, sheds and storage buildings can be added to the visit, and we tell you what to move or trim so pests have less cover near the house.
