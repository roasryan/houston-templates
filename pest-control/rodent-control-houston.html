Rodent control in Houston

Rodent control in Houston starts with finding how they get in. We inspect the roofline, attic, garage and foundation, set traps and seal the gaps. A report after each visit shows what we found.

Signs of rats and mice

  • Scratching in walls or attic at night
  • Droppings in drawers or the pantry
  • Gnawed wires, boxes or food bags
  • Grease marks along walls
  • A musty smell in the attic or garage
  • Chewed insulation
  • Pets reacting to sounds in the wall

How rodent control works

  1. 1InspectWe check the roofline, vents, garage, pipes and foundation.
  2. 2TrapTraps and stations go in the attic, garage or interior runs.
  3. 3Seal entry pointsGaps are sealed with steel wool, mesh and sealant.
  4. 4Follow upWe check traps, remove catches and advise on cleanup.

Good to know in Houston

Roof rats are common in Houston, living in trees and attics, and they use power lines and fences to reach rooftops. Floods and storms can push rodents into homes. Trimming trees back from the roof helps keep them out.

Pest control companies in Texas are licensed by the Texas Department of Agriculture (TDA) Structural Pest Control Service, and technicians work under a certified applicator.
