What changes the price

  • Guest count.
  • Menu level.
  • Hours of service.
  • Travel distance.
  • Add-ons like drinks and late-night snacks.
  • Date, season and weekday.

Ways to save

  • Book on a weekday or off-season.
  • Pick the simple taco bar.
  • Keep the party inside Beltway 8.
  • Get a firm guest count early.
  • Ask for the menu and price in writing.
