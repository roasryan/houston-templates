Good to know in Houston

Houston parties are often outdoors, and summer heat and rain matter. The truck has a serving awning, so light rain is fine, and we pause service during lightning. Pick a spot with shade for guests and a flat place for the truck.

Texas now licenses mobile food vendors through the Department of State Health Services (DSHS), starting July 1, 2026. City of Houston permits stopped counting after that date. The Houston Fire Marshal still inspects propane and cooking equipment.

What do you need at my venue?

A flat spot for the truck and a 30-amp outlet. If there is no outlet, we run a quiet generator about 20 feet from your guests.

Can you feed vegetarians?

Yes. We bring mushroom and rajas tacos, and corn tortillas are gluten-free.

What if it rains?

The truck has a serving awning, so light rain is fine. If there is lightning we pause service until it passes.
