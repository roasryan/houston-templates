Good to know in Houston

Houston weddings often run outdoors, and heat and rain can change plans. Ask your venue about shade, power and a rain plan. Fall and spring Saturdays book fastest, so ask early.

Mobile food vendors in Texas need a DSHS license. Some venues also require proof of insurance and a food manager, so ask your venue and we send what it needs.
