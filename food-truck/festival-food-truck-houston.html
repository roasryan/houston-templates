Good to know in Houston

Houston outdoor events mean heat, humidity and pop-up storms. We plan for shade, water and a lightning pause. Spring and fall are festival seasons, so dates fill early.

Texas licenses mobile food vendors through DSHS, and some events also require a temporary event permit and proof of insurance. Fire rules apply to propane and cooking equipment.
