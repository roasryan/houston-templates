Questions

How much does it cost to hire a taco truck in Houston?

Our private party taco bar starts at $1,350 for 50 or more guests, about $22 to $34 a guest depending on the menu. Office lunches have a $1,000 minimum.

What do you need at my venue?

A flat spot for the truck and a 30-amp outlet. If there is no outlet, we run a quiet generator about 20 feet from your guests.

Do you have a license to serve food?

Yes. In Houston, food trucks are permitted by the Houston Health Department (demo license), and our truck is inspected and our team has food handler cards.

What if it rains?

The truck has a serving awning, so light rain is fine. If there is lightning we pause service until it passes.

Can you feed vegetarians and people with allergies?

Yes. We bring mushroom and rajas tacos, and corn tortillas are gluten-free. The fryer is shared, so tell us about celiac guests.
