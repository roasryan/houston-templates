Kitchen and bathroom cleaning in Houston

Kitchen and bathroom cleaning in Houston focuses on the two rooms that get dirtiest. We cut grease, scrub soap scum, clean grout and sanitize surfaces. It is a good option when you do not need the whole house cleaned.

When to book a kitchen and bath clean

  • Grease on the stovetop, hood or cabinets
  • Soap scum in the tub or shower
  • Dark or mildewed grout
  • Dirty fridge shelves or oven
  • Water spots on glass and faucets
  • Smelly drains or trash area
  • You have a visit coming and need the main rooms ready
