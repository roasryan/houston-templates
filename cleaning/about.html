About Bayou Sparkle Home Cleaning

Bayou Sparkle Home Cleaning is a demo business built to show how a Houston cleaning website can work. The company, phone number and prices are examples and are not a real service.

Here is how the demo business works. Cleaners are background-checked and keys are logged. You get a price in writing before the first visit. If the team misses the arrival window, you get a credit, and if something is missed, the team re-cleans for free within 24 hours.

The demo service area covers Houston, The Heights, Montrose, Garden Oaks, Oak Forest, River Oaks, Bellaire and Midtown. Recurring, deep, move-out and Airbnb cleans are all offered.
