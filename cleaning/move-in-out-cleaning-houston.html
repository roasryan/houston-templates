Move-in and move-out cleaning in Houston

Move-in and move-out cleaning in Houston gets an empty home clean for the next person, or for you. We follow a checklist that matches what landlords look for. That helps you get your deposit back and start fresh in a new place.

When to book a move clean

  • Your lease ends and you want your deposit back
  • You are selling and want the home to show well
  • You are moving into a place that was not cleaned well
  • A tenant left and the unit needs a turnover
  • Cabinets and drawers are empty and ready to clean
  • The oven and fridge need a full clean
  • Carpets and floors need a final clean

Good to know in Houston

Many Houston landlords and apartment complexes charge cleaning fees from deposits, and move-out standards are strict. Moves cluster at the end of the month and in summer, so book a few days ahead. After heavy storms, floor and grout dirt can also build up quickly in empty homes.
