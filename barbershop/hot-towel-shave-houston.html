Hot towel shave in Houston, TX

A hot towel shave in Houston is a slow, close shave with warm towels, lather and a straight razor. At Third Ward Fade Co. it is $45, and every client gets a fresh blade. Many guys book it before a wedding or just to relax.

Why men book a hot towel shave

  • A close shave that lasts longer than a razor at home
  • A fix for razor bumps when done correctly
  • A treat before a wedding, a date or a big day
  • Warm towels open the skin so the shave is gentler
  • A good way to clean up a beard neckline
  • It is quiet time in a busy week

Good to know in Houston

After a shave in Houston’s heat and sun, skin can burn easily. Use a light moisturizer with SPF the next morning and skip a hot gym session for a few hours.

Barbers must hold a TDLR license, and Texas rules require a new single-use blade for each client.

Questions about hot towel shaves

How much is a hot towel shave in Houston?

Usually $35 to $65. Ours is $45.

Is a straight razor shave safe?

With a licensed barber and a fresh blade, yes. Tell your barber if you have bumps, cuts or sensitive skin.

How long does it take?

Plan on about 30 to 40 minutes.

Will it stop razor bumps?

Not always. Shaving with the grain and a warm towel can help, but results vary by skin.
