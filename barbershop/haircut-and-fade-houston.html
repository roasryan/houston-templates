Good to know in Houston

Houston heat and humidity make hair grow out soft and puffy fast, so most people keep a fade sharp for two to three weeks. Many people in Third Ward book a short line-up halfway through to stay clean between cuts. Walk-in lines get long on Saturdays, so booking saves real time.

Texas requires barbers to hold a TDLR barber license and the shop to hold a TDLR establishment license. A new neck strip and cleaned tools are required for every client.

Questions about haircuts and fades

How much is a haircut and fade in Houston?

Shops in Houston usually charge about $25 to $50. Ours start at $35, and a skin fade is $40.

How long does a fade take?

Plan on about 40 minutes. A skin fade with a razor finish can run a little longer.

What should I tell my barber?

Say the fade height (low, mid or high), how short the sides should be and how much length to keep on top. A photo helps a lot.

How often should I get a fade?

Most fades look sharpest for two to three weeks. A line-up in between keeps the edges clean.
