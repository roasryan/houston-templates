Kids’ haircut in Houston, TX

A kids’ haircut in Houston should be calm, quick and good-looking. At Third Ward Fade Co. barbers cut for ages 12 and under for $25, and take it slow if your child is nervous. Seniors get the same $25 price.

Tips before you come

  • Book a time after a nap and a snack
  • Tell the barber if it is your child’s first cut
  • Bring a photo of the style they want
  • Sensory needs or fears are fine to mention
  • Kids 12 and under get the kids’ price
  • Seniors can book the same $25 cut
  • Let your child sit in the chair first if they are shy
