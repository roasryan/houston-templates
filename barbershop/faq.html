Barbershop questions, answered

General

How much is a haircut in Houston?

A good barbershop fade or taper in Houston usually runs $25 to $50. Ours start at $35, a skin fade is $40 and a haircut with a beard trim is $50.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins go in order. Booking online holds your chair so you skip the wait, especially on Saturdays.

What if I’m late or can’t make it?

Cancel at least 4 hours ahead and there’s no charge. Saturday no-shows lose the $10 deposit and a $20 fee applies.

How often should I get a fade?

Most fades look sharpest for 2 to 3 weeks. A line-up in between keeps it clean for about $20.

Are your barbers licensed?

Yes. Texas requires a TDLR barber license and a licensed shop. Every barber’s license is posted at the front desk, and licenses renew every 2 years.

Haircuts and fades

How much is a haircut and fade in Houston?

Shops in Houston usually charge about $25 to $50. Ours start at $35, and a skin fade is $40.

How long does a fade take?

Plan on about 40 minutes. A skin fade with a razor finish can run a little longer.

What should I tell my barber?

Say the fade height (low, mid or high), how short the sides should be and how much length to keep on top. A photo helps a lot.

How often should I get a fade?

Most fades look sharpest for two to three weeks. A line-up in between keeps the edges clean.

Skin fades

How much is a skin fade in Houston?

Shops usually charge about $30 to $55. Ours is $40.

Will a skin fade hurt or cause bumps?

It should not hurt. Razor bumps can happen if your skin is sensitive, so tell your barber and ask for a lighter pass and a calming balm.

How long does a skin fade last?

The sharp look usually lasts about two weeks before the sides fuzz up.

Can I get a skin fade with curly hair?

Yes. The barber blends the curls on top into the skin on the sides. Come in with your hair dry so the shape is easy to see.

Haircut and beard

How much is a haircut and beard trim in Houston?

Usually $40 to $70. Ours is $50 for both.

How long should my beard be before I come?

A week or two of growth is plenty for a trim. For a full shape, a month of growth gives your barber more to work with.

Can you fix a patchy beard?

A barber can shape it so the patches show less. Results depend on how your beard grows.

Is the hot towel included?

Yes. The hot towel finish is part of our haircut and beard.

Line-ups and shape-ups

How much is a line-up in Houston?

Usually $12 to $30. Ours is $20.

How often should I get a line-up?

Once a week keeps the edges tight. Every two weeks works for most people.

Is a line-up the same as a shape-up?

Yes. The words mean the same thing: a clean outline of the hairline and neckline.

Can I get one without a haircut?

Yes. A line-up is a stand-alone service.

Kids’ and senior cuts

How much is a kids’ haircut in Houston?

Usually $18 to $35. Ours is $25 for ages 12 and under.

What age is a kid’s cut?

Ours covers ages 12 and under.

What if my child is scared?

Tell us when you book. The barber will go slowly and let your child stop if needed.

Do seniors get a discount?

Seniors pay the same $25 as kids.

Hot towel shaves

How much is a hot towel shave in Houston?

Usually $35 to $65. Ours is $45.

Is a straight razor shave safe?

With a licensed barber and a fresh blade, yes. Tell your barber if you have bumps, cuts or sensitive skin.

How long does it take?

Plan on about 30 to 40 minutes.

Will it stop razor bumps?

Not always. Shaving with the grain and a warm towel can help, but results vary by skin.

Cost

Is tipping expected at a Houston barbershop?

Most people tip 15 to 20 percent. It is not included in the price.

Are walk-in cuts cheaper?

Usually the price is the same. Booking saves time, not money.

Does the price change by hair type?

Some shops charge more for long, thick or heavily textured hair. Ask when you book.

Do you charge for no-shows?

Saturday bookings take a $10 deposit that goes toward your cut. Cancel at least 4 hours ahead.
