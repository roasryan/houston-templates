Skin fade in Houston, TX

A skin fade in Houston takes the sides and back down to the skin, with a smooth blend into the hair above. It is a sharp, high-contrast look, and it takes a barber with a steady hand. Our skin fade is $40 and takes about 40 minutes.

Is a skin fade right for you

  • You like a very short, very clean look on the sides
  • You want strong contrast between the sides and the top
  • You wear a beard and want it to fade into the skin
  • You do not mind coming back every two weeks
  • Your skin handles close shaving without much bumping
  • You want a look that works in the Houston heat
  • You want a bald fade, a drop fade or a burst fade

How a skin fade is done

  1. 1Pick the shapeChoose a low, mid, high or drop fade and where it should start.
  2. 2Clipper blendYour barber steps down through the guards to build a smooth gradient.
  3. 3Zero and foil workThe bottom of the fade is taken down to the skin with a zero blade or foil shaver.
  4. 4Razor edgeThe lineup and neckline are finished with a razor for a crisp border.
  5. 5AftercareYou get a warm towel or balm to calm the skin.

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s humidity can make freshly shaved skin feel tight or bumpy. A warm towel, a clean blade and a light, alcohol-free balm after the cut help. Skin fades show growth fast, so a lot of regulars come in every two weeks.

A new razor blade and neck strip are used on every client, as Texas health rules require, and barbers must hold a TDLR license.

Questions about skin fades

How much is a skin fade in Houston?

Shops usually charge about $30 to $55. Ours is $40.

Will a skin fade hurt or cause bumps?

It should not hurt. Razor bumps can happen if your skin is sensitive, so tell your barber and ask for a lighter pass and a calming balm.

How long does a skin fade last?

The sharp look usually lasts about two weeks before the sides fuzz up.

Can I get a skin fade with curly hair?

Yes. The barber blends the curls on top into the skin on the sides. Come in with your hair dry so the shape is easy to see.
