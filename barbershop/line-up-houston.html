Line-up and shape-up in Houston

A line-up in Houston is a quick clean-up of your hairline, temples and neckline, so your cut looks fresh between full haircuts. At Third Ward Fade Co. it takes about 15 minutes and costs $20. It is the cheapest way to look sharp.

When to book a line-up

  • Your cut is only 10 days old but the edges look fuzzy
  • You have an interview, a date or an event this week
  • You do not need a full haircut yet
  • You want your beard line cleaned up
  • You keep a longer top and just want the edges sharp
  • You are a member and your line-ups are free
  • You want the hairline shape fixed, not changed

How a line-up works

  1. 1Pick the lineStraight, natural or rounded. Your barber shows you where it will sit.
  2. 2Outline with trimmersThe hairline, temples and sideburns are outlined.
  3. 3Razor clean-upA razor takes off the stray hairs for a crisp border.
  4. 4Neckline and beardThe neckline is squared or tapered and the beard line is cleaned up if you want it.
  5. 5FinishA brush-off and a quick check in the mirror.

Good to know in Houston

Houston humidity makes hair swell and edges blur fast, so many people book a line-up every week. It is also the fix for a cut that gets sweaty and fuzzy under a hat or helmet.

Questions about line-ups and shape-ups

How much is a line-up in Houston?

Usually $12 to $30. Ours is $20.

How often should I get a line-up?

Once a week keeps the edges tight. Every two weeks works for most people.

Is a line-up the same as a shape-up?

Yes. The words mean the same thing: a clean outline of the hairline and neckline.

Can I get one without a haircut?

Yes. A line-up is a stand-alone service.
