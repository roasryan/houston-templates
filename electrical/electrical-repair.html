Electrical Repair in Houston, TX

Electrical repair in Houston covers the problems that make a house feel unsafe: breakers that trip, outlets that go dead and lights that flicker. A licensed electrician finds the cause, shows you the price in writing and fixes it.

Signs you need an electrician

  • A breaker that trips again and again
  • Outlets or switches that are warm to the touch
  • Flickering or dimming lights when the AC starts
  • A burning smell near the panel or an outlet
  • Buzzing from a switch or the panel
  • Sparks or shock when you plug something in
  • Outlets that do not work after a storm or surge
  • Aluminum wiring or an old panel you are unsure about

Good to know in Houston

Houston homes face frequent power surges from storms and grid swings, and the heat in attics makes old wire insulation brittle. Older homes in the Heights, Montrose and Third Ward may have Federal Pacific or Zinsco panels, aluminum branch wiring or ungrounded outlets. After floods, any wet electrical part must be checked before you restore power.

Electrical work for hire in Texas must be done by electricians licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). The City of Houston requires a permit for many repairs, and apprentices must work under a licensed electrician.

Questions about electrical repair

Why does my breaker keep tripping?

An overloaded circuit, a short or a failing breaker are the usual reasons. Resetting it again and again can hide a real hazard, so call an electrician.

Is a warm outlet dangerous?

Yes. A warm outlet often means a loose connection, which can overheat. Stop using it and call us.

Can you work on an old Federal Pacific panel?

We can inspect it and quote a replacement. These panels have a known history of breakers that fail to trip.

Do you handle storm damage?

Yes. If your meter base or mast is damaged, the utility may need to disconnect power first. We help coordinate it.
