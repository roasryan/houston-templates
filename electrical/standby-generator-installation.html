Standby Generator Installation, Houston

Standby generator installation in Houston keeps your AC, fridge and lights running when the grid goes down. We size the unit, handle the electrical and gas permits, run the gas line and coordinate with CenterPoint.

Why Houston homes install standby generators

  • You lost power for days after a storm like Hurricane Beryl in 2024
  • You work from home or run medical equipment
  • Food and AC matter more than a portable generator can cover
  • You want power that starts by itself
  • You have a gas line already at the house
  • You are tired of running cords during an outage
  • Your home insurance or neighbors suggest it
  • You want a transfer switch for safety

Good to know in Houston

In Houston, many outages come from hurricanes and storm lines that knock out power for days. A generator needs gas pressure that your meter may not provide, and CenterPoint may need to upgrade the meter first. Place the pad above flood height and away from windows.

Generators need an electrical permit and a gas permit in the City of Houston, and the work must be done by TDLR-licensed electricians and a licensed gas piper. CenterPoint coordination is required.

Questions about standby generators

How big a generator do I need?

Houston homes with central AC usually need 18 to 24 kW for whole-home coverage. We size it by your actual loads.

Where does the generator go?

On a pad at least 5 feet from openings and in line with local rules. HOAs may also have say.

Does it run on natural gas?

Most do. Propane is an option when no gas line is available.

How long does the process take?

Permits, gas meter and inspection can take weeks. After an outage, lead times grow.
