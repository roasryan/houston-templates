Electrical repair in Montrose, Houston

Montrose is known for its restaurants, art and shaded streets of 1920s to 1950s bungalows, plus newer townhomes squeezed onto old lots. That mix makes electrical work in Montrose both common and varied.

Older bungalows in Westmoreland and Avondale often have mixed wiring from many decades of changes. We see ungrounded outlets, spliced wires in attics, fuse panels and aluminum branch circuits that were used in the 1960s and 1970s. Each one can be made safe, but it takes a careful look.

Montrose has a lot of renovation work too. Owners open walls to add kitchens, baths and second stories, and an electrician has to match old wiring with new code. Arc-fault and GFCI breakers, grounding and panel space are typical topics.

After storms, Montrose’s old trees can bring down service lines and masts. We repair damaged masts and meter bases, and we coordinate with CenterPoint when power must be cut. Permits are pulled for upgrades, and inspections are scheduled for you. Ask for a written list of every wiring issue we find, ranked by risk, so you can plan the work over time.
