Lighting and Ceiling Fan Installation

Ceiling fan and lighting installation in Houston adds comfort and cuts AC cost. A good fan helps your AC work less, and good lights make a room safer. We install fans, recessed lights, dimmers and outdoor lights.

When to add lighting or a ceiling fan

  • A room feels dark or has one weak light
  • You want recessed lights in a kitchen or living room
  • A fan wobbles or hums
  • A room is stuffy in summer
  • Your porch or yard is dark at night
  • Old light fixtures flicker or buzz
  • You want dimmers or smart switches
  • You need a fan-rated box in the ceiling

Good to know in Houston

Houston homes get a big comfort boost from ceiling fans, since moving air makes a room feel several degrees cooler. In attic spaces over 130° in summer, recessed lights must be rated for insulation contact. Outdoor lights need wet-rated fixtures because of rain and humidity.

Lighting and fan installs must be done by a TDLR-licensed electrician. Adding new circuits can need a City of Houston permit.

Questions about lighting and fans

Do I need a special box for a ceiling fan?

Yes. A fan-rated box holds the weight and the motion. A normal light box can fall.

How many recessed lights do I need?

A rule of thumb is one for every 4 to 6 feet of ceiling. We plan the layout with you.

Can you hang a heavy fixture?

Yes. We add braces in the ceiling when needed.

Do LED lights save money?

Yes. They use less power and run cooler, which also helps your AC.
