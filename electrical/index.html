Questions

How much does a panel upgrade cost in Houston?

Most 200 amp panel upgrades run $2,500 to $4,500. The price depends on the meter base, the distance to the panel and permit fees. We give you a written price first.

How much is a whole-home generator?

Standby generators with a transfer switch usually run $9,000 to $18,000 installed. Size, gas line length and permits change the price.

Do I need a permit for electrical work?

Panel and service upgrades, generators and new circuits need a City of Houston permit and an inspection. Small swaps like an outlet usually do not. We pull permits for you.

What is a CenterPoint disconnect?

On a panel upgrade, CenterPoint has to cut power at the meter and reconnect after the city inspection. It can add a few days, and we keep you updated at each step.

Are you licensed?

Yes. All work is done by electricians licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Apprentices always work under a master electrician.
