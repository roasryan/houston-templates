EV Charger Installation in Houston

EV charger installation in Houston means adding a dedicated 240-volt circuit and a safe charger in your garage or driveway. We check your panel first, pull the City permit and finish the job in about a day.

Is your home ready for a home EV charger

  • You bought or leased an EV or plug-in hybrid
  • A standard outlet charges your car too slowly
  • Your panel has space for a 40 or 50-amp breaker
  • The car parks close to the panel or in a garage
  • You want to charge off-peak at night
  • Your home is on a 100-amp service and needs a load check
  • You want a charger that works with your car’s app
  • You want the install permitted and inspected

How a charger install works

  1. 1Home checkWe look at your panel, wire path and where the car parks.
  2. 2Choose the chargerWe help you pick a Level 2 charger that fits your car and budget, or install the one you bought.
  3. 3PermitWe pull the City of Houston EV charging permit. No utility disconnect is needed for most installs.
  4. 4InstallWe mount the charger, run the wire and add a dedicated breaker.
  5. 5Test and inspectionWe test charging, show you the app and the city inspects.

Good to know in Houston

A garage in Houston can reach 100° in summer, so we use wire and chargers rated for heat. Detached garages and outdoor driveways need weatherproof gear and sometimes a trench. If your garage can flood, mount the charger higher on the wall.

The City of Houston requires an electrical permit and inspection for a Level 2 EV charger, and the work must be done by a TDLR-licensed electrician. Some utilities offer rebates; check with your provider.
