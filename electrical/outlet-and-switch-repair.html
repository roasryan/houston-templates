Outlet and Switch Repair, Houston

Outlet and switch repair in Houston is a common fix that should not be a DIY. A dead outlet or loose switch can mean a loose wire behind the wall. We find the cause, repair or replace it and test the whole circuit.

Signs an outlet or switch needs work

  • An outlet that does not work
  • A plug that falls out or feels loose
  • A switch that clicks but does nothing
  • A GFCI that will not reset
  • Discolored or hot cover plates
  • Buzzing or crackling in the wall
  • Two-prong outlets in an older room
  • You want USB or tamper-resistant outlets for kids

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s humidity corrodes outlets in garages and outdoor boxes, so we use weather-resistant covers and GFCI protection outside. After flooding or a roof leak, outlets can be wet inside the wall and look fine. Have them checked before you use them.

Outlet work for hire must be done by a TDLR-licensed electrician. Kitchens, baths, garages and exteriors need GFCI protection under code.

Questions about outlets and switches

Why does my GFCI keep tripping?

Moisture, a worn device or a ground fault on the circuit can cause it. An electrician can find which one.

Can I replace an outlet myself?

Homeowners may do some work on their own home, but wiring errors cause fires. We suggest a licensed electrician.

Do you install USB outlets?

Yes. They are a good fit for kitchens, bedrooms and offices.

Is a loose outlet dangerous?

It can be. Loose connections create heat. Fix them early.
