Good to know in Houston

Houston traffic can slow a delivery, so we plan the route and leave early. Office buildings downtown and in the Galleria often have loading dock rules, so tell us where to deliver. Summer heat means food stays in a cooled van until it arrives.

Food comes from a permitted kitchen that follows City of Houston Health Department rules. Hot food is held at 135°F or higher and cold food at 41°F or lower.
