Good to know in Houston

Houston heat makes cold drinks and ice go fast, so plan extra ice. Outdoor bars need shade. Many venues have their own alcohol rules, so check yours first.

Texas requires TABC seller-server training for people who serve alcohol, and our bartenders hold it. Guests must be 21 or older to be served.
