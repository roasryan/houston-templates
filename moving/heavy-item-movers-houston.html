Good to know in Houston

Many Houston garages hold heavy items like safes, gyms and freezers, often on slab floors with a step down. Humid air can cause rust on steel items, so we wrap and dry them. Narrow older doorways in the Heights and Montrose can make a large piece harder to carry.

Texas movers of household goods are regulated by the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV). You should get a written proposal and the TxDMV rights brochure before anything is loaded.
