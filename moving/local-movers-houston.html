Local movers in Houston

Local movers in Houston should give you a written price before they touch a box. We send a not-to-exceed proposal, load with pads and wrap, and sign an inventory at pickup and delivery. You know the most you can pay before move day.

When to hire local movers

  • You have a couch, bed or dresser too heavy to carry safely
  • You are moving across Houston or to a nearby suburb
  • You want a fixed ceiling on cost
  • You cannot take time to rent a truck and find help
  • You have stairs, a long carry or a tight elevator
  • You are moving at month-end and need a firm date
  • You want your furniture wrapped and your floors protected

Good to know in Houston

Houston moves are hot, humid and often rained out. Crews start early in summer to avoid the heat, and rain can slow loading and risk water on furniture. Traffic on I-10, I-45 and Highway 59 can change drive time, so we plan the route around rush hours.

Texas movers of household goods are regulated by the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV). You should get a written proposal and the TxDMV rights brochure before anything is loaded.

Questions about local moves

How much do local movers cost in Houston?

Prices vary by crew size and time. Local crews often charge by the hour for movers and a truck, and customers on review sites have reported about $180 for the first two hours for two movers. Get a written proposal.

Is the price a flat rate?

We give a not-to-exceed proposal. You pay the actual time up to that limit.

Do I tip the crew?

It is not required. Many customers tip for hard days.

What should I have ready?

Boxes packed and taped, furniture emptied of loose items and a clear path to the truck.
