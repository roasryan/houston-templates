What movers cost in Houston

Moving cost in Houston usually depends on crew size, hours and distance. Local crews often charge by the hour, plus a truck fee. Here are typical ranges and what changes them.

Ranges are for planning only. Your written price comes after we see the job.

What changes the price

  • Crew size and hours.
  • Distance and drive time.
  • Stairs and long carries.
  • Packing and supplies.
  • Heavy or special items.
  • Date. Month-end and summer are busiest.

Ways to save

  • Book weekdays and mid-month dates.
  • Pack and label before the crew arrives.
  • Clear a path to the truck and reserve parking.
  • Sell or donate what you will not use.
  • Get a written not-to-exceed proposal before you book.

Cost questions

Why do movers charge by the hour?

Time is the main cost: crew and truck time. A not-to-exceed proposal caps the total.

Are there hidden fees?

A written proposal lists truck, travel and supplies. Changes on move day need a signed amendment.

Is insurance included?

Movers carry cargo insurance, and valuation coverage is separate. Ask what each covers.

Should I get more than one quote?

Yes. Compare proposals, not just hourly rates.
