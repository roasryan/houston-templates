Questions

How much do movers cost in Houston?

Local movers usually charge $130 to $230 per hour for a truck and 2 to 4 movers. A 2 bedroom apartment often takes 3 to 5 hours. You get a written not-to-exceed price first.

Do you charge for the truck?

Yes. We add a truck and travel fee, shown on your quote. Time starts when we arrive and ends when we finish unloading.

Are you licensed to move?

Yes. Household goods movers in Texas must register with the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV) and show their number on trucks and ads (demo license). We carry cargo insurance too.

What if something gets broken?

Tell us within 90 days and we file a claim. We respond in writing within 23 days. Full value protection with no deductible is an optional add-on.

What should I do before move day?

Pack boxes, label rooms, and set aside valuables. Our checklist in your portal walks you through it.
