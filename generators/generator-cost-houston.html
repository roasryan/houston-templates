What a standby generator costs in Houston

Generator prices in Houston depend on size, gas, wiring and permits. These are typical ranges, not quotes. A licensed installer should see your home and give you one price in writing.

What changes the price

  • Generator size in kW
  • Whole-home or essentials coverage
  • Distance from the pad to the meter and panel
  • Gas meter upgrade needs
  • Permits, HOA rules and pad prep
  • Brand, warranty length and add-ons like Wi-Fi monitoring

Ways to save

  • Buy before hurricane season, not the week after a storm.
  • Use load management instead of jumping to a bigger unit.
  • Place the pad close to the meter and panel when setbacks allow.
  • Ask for one written price that includes gas, wiring and permits.
  • Keep up with yearly service so small issues don’t become big repairs.

Cost questions

Why do generator prices jump after a storm?

Demand spikes, units sell out and permit and meter lines grow. Lead times can stretch for months.

Is a portable generator cheaper?

Yes, up front. But it won’t start by itself, needs gas cans and can’t safely run central AC without a switch.

What does it cost to run?

It depends on the size, the load and gas prices. A larger unit at full load burns much more gas per hour than at half load.

Are these your prices?

No. They are typical Houston ranges. Prices marked as ours come from our own price list.
