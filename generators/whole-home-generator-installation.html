Whole-Home Generator Installation in Houston

Whole-home generator installation in Houston keeps your AC, fridge, lights and Wi-Fi running when the grid goes down. The unit starts by itself within seconds. We size it, pull the permits, run the gas and wire it to a transfer switch.

Signs a whole-home generator makes sense

  • You lost power for days after Hurricane Beryl in 2024
  • You sat through rolling outages in Winter Storm Uri in 2021
  • Someone at home relies on medical equipment or a CPAP
  • You work from home and can’t lose internet
  • A hot house is unsafe for kids, older family or pets
  • You already have natural gas at the house
  • You are tired of hauling a portable generator and gas cans
  • You want power that switches over with no one home

Good to know in Houston

Houston outages often come from hurricanes, derechos and storm lines that drop trees on power lines. After Hurricane Beryl in July 2024, many homes went a week or more without power in the summer heat. Houston clay soil shifts, so the unit needs a level, solid pad. In flood-prone areas, we set it as high as the site allows.

In the City of Houston, a standby generator needs a separate electrical permit and plumbing permit, plus pad, site and gas inspections. The city guide sets a 5 foot minimum from openings and combustible walls and 2 feet from the property line.

Questions about whole-home generators

How long can a whole-home generator run?

On natural gas, it can run as long as gas keeps flowing. It needs oil checks during a long outage, often every few days of nonstop use.

Will it run my central AC?

Yes, if it is sized for it. Many Houston homes need 22 kW or more, or load management, to run two AC units.

Is it loud?

It sounds like a running lawn mower from a short distance. Newer units are quieter, and the city noise rules still apply.

Can I finance it?

Many owners do. We can show options, but read the terms closely before you sign.
