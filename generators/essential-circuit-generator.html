Essential-Circuit Generators in Houston

An essential-circuit generator in Houston powers the things you can’t go without, like the fridge, lights, Wi-Fi and one AC unit. It costs less than whole-home power and still starts by itself. We help you pick which circuits matter most.

Signs an essentials-only setup fits you

  • You want backup power but whole-home is over budget
  • Your home is under 2,000 sq ft with one AC unit
  • You mostly care about food, light, internet and one cool room
  • Your gas meter is small and an upgrade is hard
  • You have a CPAP or medical device to keep running
  • You work from home and need a desk and Wi-Fi
  • You plan to add more coverage later
  • You rent out a home and want basic backup

Good to know in Houston

In a Houston summer outage, the house heats up fast. Keeping even one AC running on a bedroom zone makes a long outage livable. Smaller bungalows in the Heights and Montrose often fit an essentials setup well, since the gas meter and panel are usually small.

Essential-circuit generators need the same City of Houston electrical and plumbing permits and inspections as whole-home units. The transfer switch must be listed and installed by a TDLR-licensed electrician.

Questions about essential-circuit generators

Can a small generator run my AC?

One smaller AC, yes, if the unit is sized for it. A soft-start kit on the AC can help it start on a smaller generator.

Can I add more circuits later?

Often, yes. We plan the switch and wiring so it can grow.

Is essentials-only cheaper to run?

Yes. A smaller unit burns less gas per hour.

Does it still start by itself?

Yes. The transfer switch senses the outage and starts the generator.
