What we do

  • Cleaning for leather, aniline, nubuck, distressed leather and suede
  • Faded leather color restoration
  • Minor scratch and gouge repair, blended into the surrounding leather
  • Pro-applied leather protector that stays soft for the life of the leather

Why clean leather?

Body oils, dust and spills dry leather out over time. Regular cleaning keeps it soft and helps it resist cracks and tears, so your furniture lasts longer.

Leather protector

Applied in your home or office. It dries in about 6 hours, guards against water- and oil-based spills, and doesn't need to be reapplied for about 5 years.
