Questions

How much does it cost?

Carpet is $80 a room and tile and grout about $1 a square foot. Small jobs under $175 have a $175 visit minimum. Upholstery and rugs are priced by piece once Josh sees the fabric. Heavy soil, restoration or add-ons like protector can change the price, and Josh confirms it before he starts. See your price online any time.

How does tile and grout cleaning work?

Solution, agitation, heat, pressure and extraction pull dirt out of the grout lines. Sealing the grout afterward helps it resist new stains.

Are your solutions safe for kids and pets?

Josh is picky about products and buys from trusted professional suppliers. Ask him about any product, and tell him about kids, pets or allergies. A soap-free Green Clean option is available.
