House Cleaning Tips for your Houston Home

By Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner

My Pro Cleaner™ had the privilege of participating in a nationwide article arranged by Julia Weaver for Redfin Real Estate.

We joined Professional Cleaners from all over the country giving their best quick tips for "Deep Cleaning Your Home for a Tidier New Year".

As a professional carpet cleaner serving the Houston area since 2004, we feel that between professional deep cleanings, it's important for homeowners to keep their entire homes clean. A cleaner home overall will also keep your carpets cleaner throughout the year.

Our contribution to the article focussed on best practices for regular vacuuming.

For My Pro Cleaner™'s complete article on how to properly vacuum for best results, and how often to vacuum depending on your circumstances, please read our featured article on this website "Vacuuming tips for Better Cleaning".
