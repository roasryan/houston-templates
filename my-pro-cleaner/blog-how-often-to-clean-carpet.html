How Often Should You Have Your Carpets Professionally Cleaned?

By Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner

For the average home, carpet makers and the IICRC recommend hot water extraction every 12 to 18 months. Josh recommends about once a year, and never longer than 18 months.

What changes the answer

Your warrantyMany carpet warranties require regular professional steam cleaning. Keep your receipts.
PetsDirt, dander, skin oils and urine build up fast. Pet homes need cleaning more often.
KidsFood, drinks, crayons and muddy shoes. If food is allowed on the carpet, think every 6 months.
Traffic & guestsBusy homes need more. A couple with no kids or pets may be fine at 18 months.
AllergiesMore frequent cleaning means less dust, dander and allergens in the air.

Make the time between cleanings count

  • Vacuum often, at least 3 times a week with pets.
  • Brush pets to cut down on shedding.
  • Blot spills right away.
  • Use doormats and runners in busy spots.
  • Shoes off at the door.

Dirt that settles deep in carpet acts like sandpaper on the fibers, and cleaning before carpet gets very dirty gives better results. Ask Josh to suggest a schedule for your home.
