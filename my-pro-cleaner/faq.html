FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Carpet cleaning basics

What cleaning method do you use?

Hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning, with a commercial water softener. The method and solution are matched to the fiber, the soil and the stains.

How long will my carpets take to dry?

Usually about 2 hours, and often about 1 hour when Josh sets up air movers. Very dirty carpet can take longer, up to 8 hours. Airflow and humidity matter too, so run your AC and ceiling fans.

Is a pro cleaning better than a rental machine?

Yes. Pro equipment brings more heat, rinsing, extraction and drying. Rental machines often overwet and leave sticky detergent behind.

Will cleaning make my carpet get dirty faster?

Not when it's done right. A thorough rinse leaves very little residue, and residue is what attracts new dirt.

Do I need to vacuum first?

No. Every room is pre-vacuumed with a commercial vacuum with sealed, hospital-grade filtration before the deep clean.

Spots, stains & pet odors

Can you get out pet urine and odor?

Often, yes. It depends on how much there is and whether it reached the pad, subfloor or baseboards. Josh treats the source instead of covering the smell.

Why do some pet odors come back after cleaning?

Urine can soak into the pad, and humidity can bring old odor back. That can take targeted odor treatment or a deeper flush.

Can every stain come out?

No. Some stains are permanent, like bleach, dye or damaged fiber. Call before trying several store products, since some make stains harder to remove.

Safety, protector & care

Are your solutions safe for kids and pets?

Josh is picky about products and buys from trusted professional suppliers. Ask him about any product, and tell him about kids, pets or allergies. A soap-free Green Clean option is available.

Does cleaning remove carpet protector?

Foot traffic wears protector away over time. A proper cleaning doesn't strip it, and cleaning day is a good time to restore it.

Is it worth restoring protection?

Yes. Spills soak in more slowly, so you have time to blot them, and vacuuming works better.

How often should carpets be cleaned?

Most carpet makers suggest about once a year. Homes with pets, kids, allergies, light carpet or heavy traffic often need it more.

Rugs, upholstery, tile & commercial

Do you clean upholstery and delicate fabrics?

Yes. Each piece is checked for fabric type, soil and risks first, so the method won't overwet, shrink or bleed the fabric.

How does tile and grout cleaning work?

Solution, agitation, heat, pressure and extraction pull dirt out of the grout lines. Sealing the grout afterward helps it resist new stains.

Do you offer commercial carpet cleaning?

Yes, for offices, clinics, retail and similar spaces in Spring and North Houston, including maintenance plans.
