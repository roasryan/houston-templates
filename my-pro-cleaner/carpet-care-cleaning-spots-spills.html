Carpet Care 101: 7 Things We Wish Everyone Knew

By Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner

1. Know what your carpet is made of

Over 90% of carpet made today is synthetic (nylon, polypropylene/olefin or polyester). Most of the rest is wool. The fiber decides which cleaners are safe. A pro can usually tell by look and feel, or with a burn test on a loose fiber from a hidden edge.

2. Keep a spill kit ready

  • Clear, non-bleach dish soap and white vinegar
  • Plain white rags and paper towels, a spray bottle and gloves
  • Non-oily nail polish remover, gum remover and a grease/tar spot remover
  • Ice cubes and a butter knife or blunt scraper
  • Nice extras: a wet vac and a black light to find pet urine

Skip household bleach and ammonia unless the spot & spill guide says otherwise.

3. Use only white cloths

Colored rags and printed paper towels can bleed dye into your carpet.

4. Blot, don't scrub

Scrubbing frays fibers and pushes the spill deeper. Blot from the outside edge toward the center. Standing on a towel adds helpful pressure.

5. The basic spill routine

  1. Act fastStain-resistant carpet only buys you time.
  2. Remove solidsScrape gently from the outside in.
  3. AbsorbBlot with white towels until nothing transfers.
  4. Clean lightly, then rinseUse a little soap mix, then rinse with water so no sticky residue is left.
  5. DryStack white towels on the spot and weigh them down.

6. Hot water extraction is the pro standard

It's the method most carpet makers recommend, and many require it to keep your warranty valid. Done right, it leaves no residue behind.

7. Not all truck mounts are equal

A truck-mounted unit alone doesn't guarantee a good result. Stronger, well-kept equipment with good drying makes the difference.
