Professional Carpet Cleaning vs. Renting a Machine

By Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner

Renting a machine is cheaper up front. Here's what you give up.

Rental machine

  • Weak suction leaves carpet over-wet for a day or two
  • Tap water starts near 120° and drops below 100° after one pass
  • Soap residue makes carpet re-soil fast
  • Can cause mildew, yellowing and "wicking" (dirt rising back up)
  • Exhaust can blow allergens back into the room
  • Store trip, tank refills, cleanup and returning it

My Pro Cleaner

  • 33 hp truck mount with about 13 inches of lift
  • Water heated to about 240° and held there
  • Dry in under 2 hours, about 1 with air movers
  • Exhaust vents outside, not in your home
  • Soft water, so carpet isn't left stiff from Texas hard water
  • Pre-inspection, spot treatment, traffic lanes restored and pet spots treated

Carpets should be deep cleaned every 6 to 18 months depending on your home. If anyone has allergies or breathing problems, skip the rental.
