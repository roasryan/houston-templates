Questions

Do you offer commercial carpet cleaning?

Yes, for offices, clinics, retail and similar spaces in Spring and North Houston, including maintenance plans.

What cleaning method do you use?

Hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning, with a commercial water softener. The method and solution are matched to the fiber, the soil and the stains.

Will this meet my carpet warranty?

Usually. Many carpet warranties require periodic hot water extraction. Check your warranty's wording, and Josh can give you a receipt.

What makes My Pro Cleaner different?

Careful, expert service since 2004, steady investment in equipment and training, and a soft water system that helps solutions work better and leaves carpet softer.
