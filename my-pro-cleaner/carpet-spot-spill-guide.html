Spot & Spill Guide: How to Treat 40+ Carpet Stains

By Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner

Act fast, blot (never scrub), and use the right method for the spill. Find your stain in the chart, then follow that method below.

Golden rules

  • Know your carpet fiber (wool, nylon, olefin) before you treat anything.
  • Act quickly. No carpet is fully stain-proof. Stain-resistant carpet just buys you time.
  • Scrape up solids first with a spoon or dull knife.
  • Blot from the outside in. Scrubbing frays fibers and spreads the spill.
  • Use plain white towels only. Colored or printed towels can transfer dye.
  • Rinse with clean water after any soap. Leftover soap attracts dirt.
  • Dry it: stack white towels on the spot and weigh them down with a plastic jug.
  • Not sure? Call before trying several store products.

Your spill kit

  • Mild soap mix: ¼ tsp clear, non-bleach dish soap (like Dawn or clear Ivory) in 32 oz water. Avoid creamy soaps.
  • Vinegar mix: 1 part white vinegar to 1 part water.
  • Ammonia mix: 1 tbsp household ammonia in 1 cup water. Never on wool.
  • Non-oily nail polish remover, gum remover (freeze type), and a grease/tar spot remover (like Carbona or Energine).
  • Mild bleach mix: 1 part bleach to 5 parts water, only on carpet you know is 100% solution-dyed.

The methods

A · Water-based spillsBlot with a towel dampened in cool water until nothing more transfers. Mist the soap mix, blot from the outside in, then rinse with clean water and blot dry.
A-1 · Vinegar firstBlot or mist the vinegar mix on the spot, then do Method A.
A-2 · Ammonia firstBlot the ammonia mix on the spot, then do Method A with a full rinse. Not for wool or wool blends.
B · Oil-based spillsBlot up what you can. Put grease remover or non-oily polish remover on a white towel (never straight on the carpet) and blot. Repeat, then do Method A. Wear gloves and open a window.
C · Gum & waxFreeze it with ice, shatter it with something blunt, and vacuum the chips before they soften. Finish with Method B.
D · Bleach-safe stainsOnly the mild bleach mix, and only on solution-dyed carpet. If you're not 100% sure, don't.
