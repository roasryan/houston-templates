Here's what it means for your carpets, upholstery, and tile floors:

By Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner

We just made our biggest equipment upgrade ever!

I'm excited to share some news that's going to make a real difference for you and your home.

At My Pro Cleaner, we have always been dedicated to providing the highest quality cleaning services for your carpet, tile, and upholstery. To honor that commitment, we just made the most significant upgrade in our company's history!

After years of delivering quality results with our trusty equipment called ProChem Legend, we've just invested in a "beast!" It's called the Sapphire Scientific Apex 570 Truckmount — and I can't wait to tell you why this matters to you.

Let me be clear: our old Legend unit was solid. It served us well for years, and it was a reliable workhorse that many carpet cleaners still use today. It helped us build our reputation for quality service. But technology has evolved, and when we're committed to giving our clients the absolute best results, we needed to level up. This isn't just a new machine; it's a far superior way to clean your carpets, upholstery, and tile.

After using this new Apex 570 system for a few months now, I'll be honest: there's no comparison. The difference in performance is night and day, and that translates directly into better results for your home. Compared to what our competition uses, our results are truly unmatched.

Here is exactly how this new power translates into direct benefits for you:

Your carpets will dry dramatically faster. We know the single biggest inconvenience of professional carpet cleaning is the drying time. With our previous unit, customers often had to wait around 4–6 hours for their carpets to dry. We always used extra pro techniques, like specialized "dry strokes" (pushing the wand without water to recover more moisture), wand glides, special fans, and other methods to cut that drying time down. But now, thanks to the sheer power of the Apex 570, those longer waits are gone!

Why the dramatic improvement?

It comes down to extraction power. We still use all the other important techniques, but the Apex 570 pulls significantly more water out from deeper within your carpet with each pass. The key is called "lift" (suction). Lift refers to the machine's ability to pull water and soils out of your carpet. Our new system has an incredible 14 inches of lift, a significant increase compared to our previous unit's 12 inches. This means we are pulling out far more embedded moisture, resulting in dramatically faster drying times. We are seeing drying times reduced by approximately two hours. Where carpets once took 4 to 6 hours to dry, they are now drying in 2 to 3 hours! For hard surfaces like tile and grout, the drying process is practically instant.

Your carpets will actually be cleaner. The Apex 570 is widely described in the industry as a "beast," and for good reason. Our primary goal with this upgrade was to lift more embedded soil that is typically harder to reach. The Apex 570, which runs on a robust Kubota engine designed for constant use, simply doesn't let up on power. It provides consistent, deep cleaning performance throughout the entire job. This dependable, massive extraction power ensures we are not just cleaning the surface of your carpet — we are reaching deep into the fibers to remove hidden grime, contaminants, and soils that less powerful machines leave behind.

Better Heat Management means Maximum Results. Heat is a crucial tool, especially for dealing with stubborn spots and stains. The engineers who designed the Apex 570 solved a big heat problem. They created a system, complete with a specialized reserve tank, so that we would never run out of hot water. While our previous unit would sometimes drop in temperature from 235° down to 170° when cleaning tile, the new Apex 570 maintains a relentless temperature between 232° and 234°. For carpets, this sustained, high heat ensures optimal stain removal and maximum sanitation, giving you unmatched results.

Hire the Pro Cleaner that invests in excellence.

I upgraded our equipment for one simple reason: to get you better results. I wanted to deliver cleaner carpets, upholstery, and tile — with faster drying times, because I know how much you value both the quality of the clean and getting your home back to normal quickly.

Most carpet cleaners in our area are still running equipment similar to what we just upgraded from. When you hire My Pro Cleaner, you are hiring a professional who utilizes advanced, sophisticated equipment.

Customers often stop and stare at the Apex 570 itself in my van. They often say it's an impressive rig, illuminated by a beautiful blue glow. We frequently hear the comment: "I feel like I hired the right company."

Our decision to upgrade was simple: We want to be the best in the industry in Houston, Spring, The Woodlands, and surrounding communities. We are combining this powerhouse machine with specialized tools, a soft water system, knowledge of chemistry, and refined techniques — to ensure the highest quality clean.

I'm proud to bring this level of equipment to your home. When you schedule your next cleaning, you're getting measurably better results from a system that professionals in the industry call a "beast" for good reason.

We are convinced that there's no comparison — not just to our previous gear, but to other cleaners operating in the area. The investment we made is really an investment in you and the quality of service you deserve.

I can't wait for you to see — and feel — the difference.

Thank you for your continued trust in our service.
