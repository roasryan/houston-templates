Your Partner in Carpet Care

Hello, I am Josh Almanza, owner of My Pro Cleaner™, a professional carpet, tile, and upholstery cleaning business serving homeowners and businesses in Spring Texas — also The Woodlands, Humble, Tomball, and surrounding Houston Texas areas — since 2004.

As your professional partner in carpet care, and as your upholstery fabric care specialist, I constantly stay up-to-date on the craft and the chemistry of carpet, upholstery, rug, and tile cleaning — and especially spot and stain removal.

I attend nationwide annual conferences with other top experts in the carpet cleaning industry from across the country, so I can continually improve and contribute to the industry. I am dedicated to delivering the best possible results to you!

I’ve been hired by other carpet cleaners in Houston, Spring, and The Woodlands to conduct teaching workshops, and I think the best way to pass on helpful information to my clients is through my website. I hope you’ll visit my informational articles on carpet care, and that you’ll find them helpful.

I want to help you before, during, and after professional deep cleanings.

I hope you’ll see me as your trusted partner in the care of your carpeting... (... and upholstery and tile & grout, too).

Sincerely,

A little about my history...

I was first introduced to carpet cleaning in 1996 when my church told me about a cleaning company that was hiring helpers.

I found that carpet cleaning came easily for me, and I advanced to a tech position in the company in 1 week, rather than the 3 months it normally takes.

I began taking advanced classes and attending conferences and learning everything I could about carpet and upholstery fabric care from top experts in the country.

In 2004, I decided to dedicate myself to a better customer experience by investing in top-level equipment, superior cleaning solutions, and excellent craftsmanship and skill.

I started my own carpet cleaning business, called Prestige Carpet Cleaning — which eventually became My Pro Cleaner.

This is when I began to see carpet and upholstery cleaning — not as a job — but as a craft that requires an extraordinary amount of knowledge and skill to achieve extraordinary results.

My mission is to be the go-to expert for all homeowners and businesses in the Houston area who truly value their carpets and upholstery — who are concerned with the care and longevity of their carpets and furniture, and with the health and appearance of their home.

Now with over 18 years of hands-on experience in homes and businesses throughout the Houston area, I’m still dedicated to continually growing and expanding my skills, and keeping up-to-date with modern standards, equipment, and best practices.

Now, other cleaning companies in the area are hiring me to teach workshops on specialized techniques in carpet cleaning and fabric care.

In 2017, I began to learn and perfect tile and grout cleaning by investing in great equipment and by perfecting my methods. I really enjoy cleaning tile and grout, and seeing how happy my clients become when they see the difference!

Chemistry, not cheap solutions

Solutions are matched to each job. Budget cleaners often use cheap products and water them down. Josh uses products he trusts to be safe and effective, and explains why stains happen and how to prevent them.

Equipment that protects your home

Up-to-date truck-mounted hot water extraction, a water softener that helps solutions work and leaves fabric softer, and a commercial pre-vacuum with sealed, hospital-grade filtration for allergens and pet hair. Its rubber-coated wheels and bumpers protect your walls and furniture.

30-day spot & spill warranty

Spill something within 30 days of your cleaning? Qualifying carpet and upholstery spots are treated free.

On-time guarantee
