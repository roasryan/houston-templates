The story

Josh was introduced to carpet cleaning in 1996, when a local company was hiring helpers. He was promoted to technician within a week, a step that usually takes three months. He took advanced classes, attended conferences and learned from top experts in the industry.

In 2004 he started his own company, Prestige Carpet Cleaning, which later became My Pro Cleaner. Today he attends national conferences and other cleaners hire him to teach workshops. He added tile and grout cleaning in 2017.

Chemistry, not cheap solutions

Solutions are matched to each job. Budget cleaners often use cheap products and water them down. Josh uses products he trusts to be safe and effective, and explains why stains happen and how to prevent them.

Equipment that protects your home

Up-to-date truck-mounted hot water extraction, a water softener that helps solutions work and leaves fabric softer, and a commercial pre-vacuum with sealed, hospital-grade filtration for allergens and pet hair. Its rubber-coated wheels and bumpers protect your walls and furniture.

30-day spot & spill warranty

Spill something within 30 days of your cleaning? Qualifying carpet and upholstery spots are treated free.

On-time guarantee

If we arrive more than 30 minutes outside your window, you get a discount on your cleaning.

100% happiness guarantee

Josh stands behind every job. If something isn't right, tell him.
