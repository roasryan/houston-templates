Concrete patio in Spring, TX

Spring is north of Houston along I-45, with a historic district known as Old Town Spring and many subdivisions like Gleannloch Farms and Augusta Pines. Many homes have large backyards, and owners want a patio for grilling, a spa or a covered outdoor room.

Tall pines and oaks shade lots in this area, and roots can lift thin slabs. We grade away from roots where possible, add a firm base and tie rebar so the slab stays flat. Pine needles and shade also mean patios stay damp, so a broom finish gives a good grip.

Many Spring yards sit near creeks and detention areas, and some lots have drainage easements. A patio cannot block the path of water. We check your survey, slope the slab away from the house and keep it out of easements.

Gas and utility lines run through many back lots, so we call 811 and wait for the marks before we dig. A backyard patio normally needs no City permit, but your HOA may need to see a drawing. We list the price per square foot, the finish and any tear-out in writing.
