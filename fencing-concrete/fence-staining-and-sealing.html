Fence Staining and Sealing, Houston

Fence staining and sealing in Houston protects wood from sun and rain. A fresh stain can add years to a fence. We stain after the wood dries, so the finish soaks in and lasts.

When to stain or seal a fence

  • The wood looks gray or faded
  • Water soaks in instead of beading
  • Mildew or green stains on boards
  • Boards are splitting or cupping
  • A new fence has dried for a few weeks
  • Old stain is peeling
  • You want a color that matches the house
  • You bought a house with a weathered fence

How staining works

  1. 1Check the woodNew wood needs to dry before stain. Old wood needs cleaning.
  2. 2Clean and prepWe wash off dirt and mildew and let the wood dry.
  3. 3Protect plants and surfacesWe cover plants, siding and concrete next to the fence.
  4. 4Apply stain and sealerWe spray and back-brush so stain goes into the grain.
  5. 5Cure and checkWe check coverage and tell you when to water the yard again.

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s heat, UV and storms break down fence stain quickly, usually in two to three years. Humidity grows mildew on shaded fences. New wood needs time to dry before it takes stain, often several weeks.

Questions about staining and sealing

When should I stain a new fence?

Wait until the wood is dry. Pressure-treated pine can take several weeks. A water drop test shows if it soaks in.

How often do I need to re-stain?

Every two to three years in Houston, depending on sun and color.

Do you stain both sides?

We can. Most customers stain their own side, and some stain both.

Can you stain a gray fence?

Yes, after cleaning. We may need a brightener first.
