What fences and concrete cost in Houston

Fence and concrete prices in Houston depend on length, size, materials and soil. These are typical ranges, not quotes. We put your exact price in writing after we measure.

What changes the price

  • Total length or square footage
  • Material: pine, cedar, steel or concrete finish
  • Tear-out and haul-away
  • Slope, trees and hard digging
  • Permits and HOA drawings
  • Ready-mix fees, such as short load, standby or weekend delivery
  • Soil and drainage

Ways to save

  • Measure and mark your property line before the quote.
  • Ask for cedar with steel posts if you plan to stay, since the extra cost pays back in years.
  • Combine a gate with a new fence instead of adding it later.
  • Stain a fence once the wood is dry to extend its life.
  • Schedule concrete in dry months to avoid delays and rush fees.

Cost questions

Why do fence prices change so much?

Lumber prices move, and each yard is different. Slope, gates, tear-out and hard digging change the total.

Do I need a permit for a fence?

In Houston a permit is only needed for fences over 8 feet, masonry fences, or in a floodplain. HOAs often have more rules.

Do you need a deposit?

Most large projects need a deposit. We list it in the written price.

Are these your prices?

No. They are typical Houston ranges from public guides. Prices marked as ours come from our price list.
