When to build a new fence

  • The old fence leans, rots at the base or has missing boards
  • You are getting a dog and need a secure yard
  • A neighbor’s fence is falling onto your side
  • You want privacy from a new neighbor or a busy road
  • A pool needs a barrier
  • You bought a house with no back fence
  • Storm damage is too widespread to patch
  • The HOA wants the fence repaired or replaced

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s clay soil swells and shrinks, which pushes posts out of line. Posts set in concrete and steel posts last longer than wood in the ground. Wind from tropical storms is hard on solid fences, and heavy rain can leave soft ground that slows digging.

Texas law requires you to call 811 at least two business days before digging. Houston requires a fence permit only for fences over 8 feet, masonry fences or those in a floodplain, but HOAs often have stricter rules.

Questions about new fences

How much does a fence cost in Houston?

Public guides list about $10 to $50 per linear foot for a privacy fence, with most full projects between $1,800 and $8,000. We give an exact price in writing after we measure.

Do I need a permit?

Not for a 6-foot wood fence in most of Houston. You do for fences over 8 feet, masonry or in a floodplain. Your HOA may also need to approve.

Who owns the fence between me and my neighbor?

It depends on the property line and local rules. Check your survey and talk to your neighbor before building.

Pine or cedar?

Cedar costs more but lasts longer in wet, hot weather. Pine is fine for a tight budget if you plan to stain it.
