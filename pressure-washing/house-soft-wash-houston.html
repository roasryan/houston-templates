House soft wash in Houston

House soft wash in Houston removes algae, mildew and dirt from siding, brick and stucco with low pressure and a cleaning mix. It is gentler than a hard blast, so paint and trim stay in place. Plants are pre-wet and protected.

Signs your house needs a wash

  • Green or black streaks on siding
  • Gray film on brick and stucco
  • Mildew around windows and soffits
  • Cobwebs and dirt under eaves
  • Rust or hard water stains
  • Dirty gutter fronts
  • Curb appeal is low before a sale

Good to know in Houston

Houston heat and humidity feed algae and mildew, and north-facing walls and shaded areas turn green fast. Many homes need a wash every one to two years. Soft washing protects paint and keeps mold from taking hold.

Wash water with soap, oil or grease should not go into storm drains under Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) rules. Washing is also a taxable service in Texas, so sales tax of 8.25% applies in Houston.
