Patio and pool deck cleaning in Houston

Patio and pool deck cleaning in Houston removes slippery algae, mildew and stains from concrete, pavers and stone. We protect your plants, pool water and furniture. Your outdoor area becomes safer and cleaner.

  • Black mildew in corners
  • Stains under furniture

Good to know in Houston

Houston humidity and shade make patios and pool decks turn green quickly. Pool decks must be cleaned carefully, since chemicals and runoff cannot go into the pool or storm drain. We wash with low-toxicity mixes and rinse away from the water.

Wash water with soap, oil or grease should not go into storm drains under Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) rules. Washing is also a taxable service in Texas, so sales tax of 8.25% applies in Houston.

Questions about patios and pool decks

Do you protect the pool water?

Yes. We cover or direct runoff away from the pool.

Will cleaning damage pavers?

Not if done at the right pressure. We use soft washing and surface cleaners.

Should I seal pavers?

Sealing helps protect color and slow stains. It is optional.

How often should I clean?

Once a year in sunny spots and twice in damp or shaded areas.
