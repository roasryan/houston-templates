Good to know in Houston

Houston storefronts see rain, traffic dirt and humidity that cloud glass quickly. Shopping centers and restaurants also get grease on pads and walkways. Washing grease needs care so it does not reach storm drains.

Commercial wash water with grease or oil often must be captured instead of sent to storm drains under TCEQ rules, and washing is a taxable service in Texas. Sales tax of 8.25% applies in Houston.
