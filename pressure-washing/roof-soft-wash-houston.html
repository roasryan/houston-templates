Roof soft wash in Houston

Roof soft wash in Houston removes black streaks, algae and moss from shingles without high pressure. We apply a cleaning mix, let it work and rinse gently. Your roof looks cleaner and lasts longer.

Signs your roof needs cleaning

  • Black streaks on shingles
  • Green moss patches
  • Dark north-facing slopes
  • Dirty look next to clean neighbors
  • Algae near gutters
  • Insurance or HOA notice
  • Home going on the market

Good to know in Houston

Houston humidity feeds roof algae, which makes the black streaks you see on many shingles. Algae holds moisture and can shorten shingle life. Hurricane season is a good reason to check for loose shingles when you wash.

Wash water with soap, oil or grease should not go into storm drains under Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) rules. Washing is also a taxable service in Texas, so sales tax of 8.25% applies in Houston.

Questions about roof soft wash

Is pressure washing a roof safe?

High pressure can harm shingles. Soft washing uses low pressure.

Will the streaks come back?

They can in a few years. Zinc strips can help slow regrowth.

Do you work on tile roofs?

Tell us the roof type and we check if soft washing fits.

Is it safe for my gutters?

Yes, we protect and rinse them.
