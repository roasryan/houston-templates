Driveway pressure washing in Houston

Driveway pressure washing in Houston lifts oil, tire marks, mildew and dirt from concrete. We use surface cleaners for even results and no wand streaks. Driveways, walks and entries look new again.

Signs your driveway needs cleaning

  • Black or green mildew patches
  • Oil stains from cars
  • Tire marks and dirt
  • Slippery surface when wet
  • Rust or fertilizer stains
  • Weeds growing in cracks
  • Curb appeal is poor before a sale

Good to know in Houston

Houston rain and shade breed algae on concrete, and car oil collects at the garage door. Washing runs from the driveway toward the street, so we plan runoff and protect storm drains. Concrete that stays damp turns slick, so cleaning also helps safety.

Wash water with soap, oil or grease should not go into storm drains under Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) rules. Washing is also a taxable service in Texas, so sales tax of 8.25% applies in Houston.

Questions about driveway and flatwork

How long until I can park?

Usually a few hours once the surface is dry.

Should I seal my driveway?

Sealing can slow stains and make cleaning easier. It is optional.
