Houston pressure washing questions, answered

General

How much does a house wash cost in Houston?

Most homes run about $250 to $600 for a soft wash, depending on size and how much green growth there is. Driveways usually run $150 to $300.

Is pressure washing safe for my siding and plants?

Yes, when it is done right. We soft wash siding at low pressure and soak and cover your plants first, then rinse them again after.

Do I have to be home?

No. We need access to a spigot, an unlocked back gate and closed windows. You get photos when we finish.

Do you charge sales tax?

Yes. Texas taxes exterior cleaning, so every price we quote already includes the 8.25% Houston sales tax.

Where does the dirty water go?

Not into the storm drain. Texas rules keep soapy, oily water out of storm drains, so we direct it to the lawn or reclaim it on business jobs.

House soft wash

Is soft washing safe for siding?

Yes. Low pressure and the right mix clean without damaging paint or siding.

Will my plants be hurt?

We pre-wet and rinse plants and protect delicate beds.

How often should I wash my house?

About every one to two years in Houston, more often in shaded areas.

Do I need to be home?

No, but we need access to an outdoor spigot.

Driveway and flatwork

Will all stains come out?

Mildew and dirt usually do. Deep oil and rust stains may lighten but not vanish.

How long until I can park?

Usually a few hours once the surface is dry.

Should I seal my driveway?

Sealing can slow stains and make cleaning easier. It is optional.

Do you wash sidewalks?

Yes, as an add-on to your driveway.

Patios and pool decks

Do you protect the pool water?

Yes. We cover or direct runoff away from the pool.

Will cleaning damage pavers?

Not if done at the right pressure. We use soft washing and surface cleaners.

Should I seal pavers?

Sealing helps protect color and slow stains. It is optional.

How often should I clean?

Once a year in sunny spots and twice in damp or shaded areas.

Fences

Will pressure damage my fence?

Too much can. We use the right tip and pressure for the wood.

When can I stain?

Wait until the wood is fully dry, often 24 to 48 hours.

Do you clean vinyl fences?

Yes, with soft wash methods.

Will my plants be safe?

We pre-wet and rinse plants.

Roof soft wash

Is pressure washing a roof safe?

High pressure can harm shingles. Soft washing uses low pressure.

Will the streaks come back?

They can in a few years. Zinc strips can help slow regrowth.

Do you work on tile roofs?

Tell us the roof type and we check if soft washing fits.

Is it safe for my gutters?

Yes, we protect and rinse them.

Gutters and storefronts

Can you clean gutters inside?

We brighten gutter exteriors. Debris cleaning can be added.

Do you work on weekly routes?

Yes. Storefront routes are weekly or monthly.

Do you clean dumpster pads?

Yes, with hot water and degreaser, with wash water captured.

Do you send proof?

Yes. We send a photo after every visit.

Cost

Is pressure washing worth it?

Regular cleaning protects paint and concrete and keeps mildew from taking hold.

Why do prices vary so much?

Size, surface, height and dirt level change the work.

Is sales tax included?

Washing is taxable in Texas. Our quotes say whether tax is included.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually. We need access to an outdoor spigot.
