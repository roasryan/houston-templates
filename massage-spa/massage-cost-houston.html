What a massage costs in Houston

A 60-minute massage in Houston usually costs about $80 to $140, and longer or specialty sessions cost more. These are typical ranges, not quotes. Our own prices are listed in the notes where we have them.

What changes the price

  • Length of the session.
  • Type of massage and therapist training.
  • Spa type: chain, boutique or resort.
  • Add-ons like hot stones, aromatherapy or cupping.
  • Weekend or evening times.
  • Membership vs. single visit.
  • Private suites and couples rooms.

Ways to save

  • Join a membership: Essential Massage is $79 a month for one 60-minute session.
  • Book a weekday slot when rates or openings are better.
  • Ask whether rollover credits are included.
  • Choose the length you need: 60 minutes is enough for most people.
  • Skip add-ons you will not use.

Cost questions

Should I tip my massage therapist?

Most people tip 15 to 20 percent. It is not included in the price.

What is your cancel policy?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for no charge. Later cancels are charged 50% and no-shows the full service.

Is massage covered by insurance?

Usually not at a day spa. A doctor-ordered therapy visit is billed differently, so ask your insurer.

What should I wear?

Undress only as far as you are comfortable. You are draped with a sheet the whole time.
