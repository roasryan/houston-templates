Good to know in Houston

Houston’s sun and humidity make skin oily in some seasons and dry under air conditioning in others. A facial every month or two can help you keep up with those swings. Use SPF every day.

Facials are cosmetic skin care and not medical treatments. Estheticians and cosmetologists are licensed by TDLR, and a body scrub is done by trained spa staff.
