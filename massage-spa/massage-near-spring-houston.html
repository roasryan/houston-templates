Massage near Spring, Houston

Spring is just east of our spa along Louetta Road and the Grand Parkway. People who live near Old Town Spring or the I-45 corridor look for a massage near Spring that is licensed, clean and easy to reach.

The area has older neighborhoods, newer subdivisions and many people who work in the Woodlands, downtown or the Energy Corridor. Long commutes on I-45 mean a lot of tension in the lower back and shoulders.

Popular choices here are 60-minute deep tissue, sports and trigger point massage and Swedish massage for stress. Many people use the Essential Massage plan, which is $79 a month for one 60-minute session, to stay regular.

I-45 and the Hardy Toll Road get slow around rush hour. Pick a time before 4 PM or after 7 PM if you can. Plan to arrive 10 minutes early on your first visit to fill out the short consultation form. We also keep your notes on file, so your next visit starts faster and your therapist remembers the pressure you like and the spots to skip.
