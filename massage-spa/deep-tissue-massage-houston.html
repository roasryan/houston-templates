Deep tissue massage in Houston, TX

A deep tissue massage in Houston uses firmer pressure on tight spots in the back, neck and shoulders. At Cypress Creek Massage & Day Spa, it is $110 for 60 minutes or $150 for 90 minutes. Your therapist adjusts pressure as you go.

Good to know before deep tissue

  • It uses slower, firmer strokes than Swedish
  • You may feel some soreness for a day
  • Tell your therapist about injuries or medications
  • Pressure should feel intense, not painful
  • Drink water before and after
  • It is a good fit for desk workers and drivers
  • Skip it if you have a recent injury or blood clot, and ask your doctor

How a deep tissue session goes

  1. 1ConsultYou say where it hurts or feels tight and how firm you like it.
  2. 2Warm-upLighter strokes warm up the muscles.
  3. 3Slow, deep workYour therapist works on tight areas with forearm, knuckles or thumbs.
  4. 4Pressure checksYou are asked often if the pressure is right.
  5. 5Stretch and finishGentle stretching and a short wind-down.

Questions about deep tissue

How much is a deep tissue massage in Houston?

Usually about $90 to $150 for 60 minutes. Ours is $110.

Will I be sore afterward?

You might be for a day. Warm water and light stretching help.

What is the difference between deep tissue and Swedish?

Deep tissue is firmer and focuses on tight spots. Swedish is lighter and more relaxing.

Can I ask for lighter pressure?

Yes, at any time. Speak up and your therapist will change it.
