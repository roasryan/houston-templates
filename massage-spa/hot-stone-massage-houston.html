Good to know in Houston

Houston spas often keep rooms cool, so hot stones feel especially nice in the winter and during air-conditioned summer days. Tell your therapist if you run hot or have sensitive skin.

Stones should be tested before use, and heat can be risky for some health conditions. Therapists must hold a TDLR license. Tell your therapist about diabetes, circulation or skin problems before the session.

Questions about hot stone massage

How much is a hot stone massage in Houston?

Usually $100 to $220 depending on length. Ours is $165 for 90 minutes.

Are hot stones safe?

They are tested for temperature first. Tell your therapist if anything feels too hot.

Who should skip hot stone?

People with certain conditions, such as diabetes, poor circulation or skin issues, should check with a doctor first.

Do the stones hurt?

They should feel warm and smooth. Speak up if the heat or pressure is too much.
