Questions

How much is a massage in Houston?

Most Houston massages run about $80 to $140 for 60 minutes, depending on the spa and the type. Ours are $95 for Swedish and $110 for deep tissue.

Do I have to take my clothes off?

Undress only as far as you’re comfortable. You are draped with a sheet the whole time, and only the area being worked on is uncovered.

What is the first-visit form for?

We ask you to fill out a short consultation form before your first massage. It covers your health and the draping rules, and we keep it on file.

Are your therapists licensed?

Yes. Texas requires massage therapists to be licensed by TDLR, and massage establishments must hold a license too. Ours is posted in the lobby.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead for no charge. Later cancels are charged 50% and no-shows the full service.
