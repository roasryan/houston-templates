Handyman in Montrose, TX

Handyman services in Montrose, Houston, cover a mix of old bungalows, new townhomes and mid-rise condos. The neighborhood sits between Westheimer, Montrose Boulevard and Taft, with an arts district feel and a lot of rental homes. Each building type needs a different kind of care.

In bungalows, we fix sticking doors, trim and drywall cracks. In townhomes, the jobs are shelves, TV mounts and punch lists after move-in. In condos, owners often need small repairs done quickly, with building rules about hours and elevator use.

Montrose is walkable, but parking can be tight. We plan tool loads and arrival windows with that in mind. Landlords and property managers also use us for turnover lists between tenants.

Many homes in the area were built before 1978, so we follow lead-safe practices for painted surfaces. Electrical and plumbing items go to licensed partners. Tell us about any HOA or building rules when you book. We bring drop cloths and shoe covers, keep noise down during quiet hours and clean up before we leave, so neighbors and building managers have nothing to complain about.
