When you need an inspection repair list done

  • The buyer sent a repair request and the deadline is close

  • The inspector flagged loose handrails or missing caulk

Good to know in Houston

Many Houston repair requests mention foundation cracks, grading, drainage and moisture. We fix cosmetic and small items, and we tell you when a foundation or roof problem needs an engineer or a specialist.

Texas has no handyman license. Electrical work needs a TDLR-licensed electrician and plumbing needs a TSBPE-licensed plumber. We send those items to licensed partners.
