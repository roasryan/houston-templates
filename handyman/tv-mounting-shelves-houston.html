TV mounting and shelves in Houston

TV mounting and shelf installation in Houston should be done into studs, not just drywall. We find the framing, level everything and hide the cables where we can. Your TV and shelves stay on the wall.

When to call for mounting and hanging

  • A new TV that is too big to lift alone
  • Shelves that sag or pull away from the wall
  • Mirrors or heavy art you are afraid to hang
  • Floating shelves with no visible brackets
  • A TV on a stand that you want off the floor
  • Kids or pets that could pull a stand over
  • Cords hanging down the wall

How a mounting visit works

  1. 1Pick the spotWe check height, viewing angle and where the studs are before drilling.
  2. 2Mount to framingBrackets go into studs or a proper anchor rated for the weight.
  3. 3Level and hangWe level the mount, set the TV or shelf and test it by hand.
  4. 4Tidy the cablesCords are routed neatly. In-wall cable runs can be priced as an add-on.

Good to know in Houston

Many Houston homes have interior walls with metal studs or old plaster, and brick fireplaces are common in older neighborhoods. A TV above a fireplace needs the heat checked first. We tell you if a spot is a bad idea before drilling.

Running new power behind a wall is electrical work and needs a licensed electrician. We mount the TV and cover the cables, but we do not add outlets.

Questions about shelves, tvs and hanging

Will the TV be safe on drywall?

Only if it is on studs or the right heavy-duty anchor. We find the studs and tell you if the wall is not strong enough.

Do you bring the mount?

We can. Add the mount to the quote or supply your own.

Can you hide the cables?

We can run cords inside the wall with a cord kit. A new outlet needs an electrician.

Can you hang a mirror on tile?

Yes, with the right bit and anchors. Tell us the tile type and we will price it.
