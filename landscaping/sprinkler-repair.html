Sprinkler Repair in Houston, TX

Sprinkler repair in Houston is mostly about broken heads, bad valves and old timers. We run each zone, find the problem, give you a price in writing and fix it under a TCEQ-licensed irrigator.

Signs your sprinklers need repair

  • Geysers or puddles in the yard
  • Dry brown patches that never green up
  • A zone that will not turn on
  • A head that sprays the wrong way
  • A valve box full of water
  • High water bills in summer
  • A controller with an old schedule
  • Sprinklers that run when it rains

Good to know in Houston

Houston clay shifts and cracks sprinkler lines, and mowers break heads in summer. A broken head can waste hundreds of gallons a day. Rain sensors keep sprinklers from running in a storm.

In Texas, installing or repairing a sprinkler system for hire must be done by a TCEQ-licensed Landscape Irrigator or a tech working under one. The license number must be on the truck and on every estimate and invoice. New systems need a rain or freeze sensor and backflow prevention.

Questions about sprinkler checks and repair

Who can repair my sprinkler in Texas?

A TCEQ-licensed Landscape Irrigator or a tech under one. Ask for the license number.

How often should I run my sprinklers?

Houston lawns often need one to two deep waterings a week in summer. Check your city’s rules.

Why is one zone brown?

A clogged head, a bad valve or a break in the line is the usual cause.

Do I need a rain sensor?

Yes, and Texas rules require one on new systems.
