Good to know in Houston

Houston’s mowing season runs about eight months, and heat indexes regularly top 105°F from June to September. Rain can force crews to reschedule, so we text you the new day. Grass cut too short in summer stresses and weeds fill in.

Lawn and landscape services are taxable in Texas. Applying weed killer or fertilizer with pesticide for hire needs a Texas Department of Agriculture (TDA) license, which a mow-and-blow crew does not hold unless stated.
