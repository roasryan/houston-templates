Lawn and landscape questions, answered

General

How much does lawn care cost in Houston?

Weekly mowing for a typical lot runs about $45 to $75 per visit. Bigger yards and tricky edges cost more. You get the price in writing before the first mow.

How much does new sod cost?

St. Augustine sod installed usually runs $1.50 to $2.50 per square foot, including grading. A pallet covers about 450 square feet.

When should I water new sod?

Water it a few times a day for the first two weeks, then taper off. We give you a simple schedule on install day.

Do you do sprinkler repair?

Yes. A Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) licensed irrigator handles repairs and installs. Every system needs a rain or freeze sensor and a backflow preventer.

Do you spray weed killer?

Yes. Our applicators hold a Texas Department of Agriculture license (demo license). We keep a record of every product we put down.

Weekly lawn care

How much is lawn care in Houston?

Public guides list about $30 to $80 per mowing visit. Your price depends on lot size and what is included.

What if it rains on my day?

We move your visit to the next dry day and text you.

Do I need to be home?

No. Just make sure gates are unlocked and pets are inside.

Do you bag clippings?

We blow them clean off hard surfaces. Bagging is possible for a fee if you ask.

Sod installation

When is the best time to lay sod in Houston?

Spring through early fall is best. St. Augustine can be laid most of the year if the ground is not frozen.

How much water does new sod need?

Water daily for the first two weeks so the roots take hold, then taper. We give you a written watering plan.

Can I walk on new sod?

Stay off for the first two weeks, except to water.

What is the sod guarantee?

If the sod fails despite correct watering, we replace it. Terms are in your written price.

Beds, mulch and plants

How deep should mulch be?

Two to three inches. More than that can trap water and harm roots.

Should I put mulch against tree trunks?

No. Leave a few inches around the trunk to prevent rot and pests.

When is the best time to plant in Houston?

Fall is best for most shrubs, since roots grow while it is mild. Spring also works.

Does mulch attract termites?

Mulch does not cause termites, but keep it a few inches from the house foundation.

Tree and shrub trimming

How often should I trim hedges?

Two to four times a year in Houston, based on the plant.

When should I trim crape myrtles?

Late winter, before new growth starts. Light shaping only.

Do you trim big trees?

We trim small trees and low limbs. For large trees we suggest a certified arborist.

Do you haul away cuttings?

Yes. It is part of the price unless we list it otherwise.

Sprinkler checks and repair

Who can repair my sprinkler in Texas?

A TCEQ-licensed Landscape Irrigator or a tech under one. Ask for the license number.

How often should I run my sprinklers?

Houston lawns often need one to two deep waterings a week in summer. Check your city’s rules.

Why is one zone brown?

A clogged head, a bad valve or a break in the line is the usual cause.

Do I need a rain sensor?

Yes, and Texas rules require one on new systems.

Weed and fertilizer program

When should I put down pre-emergent in Houston?

Around February for summer weeds and October for winter weeds.

Is it safe for pets and kids?

Keep them off the lawn until it is dry. We tell you the wait time for each product.

Why does my lawn have brown circles?

It is often brown patch fungus, common in fall and spring. A fungicide and better watering help.

Do I need to water after fertilizer?

Usually yes, lightly. We tell you what to do for each round.
