Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Houston?

Most kitchen remodels run $25,000 to $70,000 depending on size, cabinets and counters. A simple refresh with the same layout costs much less. You get a written scope and price before work starts.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

A basic bathroom remodel often runs $12,000 to $25,000. A larger one with a walk-in shower and custom tile can go above $35,000.

How long does a remodel take?

A bathroom usually takes 3 to 5 weeks and a kitchen 6 to 10 weeks. Cabinet and counter lead times are the biggest factor, and we tell you early.

Do I need a permit?

Most remodels with plumbing, electrical or walls need a City of Houston permit. We pull the permits and schedule the inspections.

Are you licensed?

Texas does not license remodelers, so we hire only licensed trades. Plumbing is done by TSBPE licensed plumbers and electrical by TDLR licensed electricians. We carry general liability insurance (demo license).
