What remodeling costs in Houston

Remodeling costs in Houston depend on size, finishes and what is hiding behind the walls. These are typical ranges, not quotes. Public cost guides list Houston averages of about $52,600 for a full kitchen and $11,900 for a full bathroom.

What changes the price

  • Size of the space
  • Finish level and brand
  • Moving plumbing, gas or walls
  • Hidden rot, mold or old wiring
  • Permits, engineering and inspections
  • Lead time and material price swings
  • Allowances for items you choose later

Ways to save

  • Keep the layout. Moving plumbing and walls is the big cost.
  • Pick all selections before demo so no late change delays the job.
  • Ask for allowances in writing and track them as you shop.
  • Do not skip a contingency, since old Houston homes hide surprises.
  • Sign a change order before any extra work so there are no surprises.

Cost questions

Why do remodels go over budget?

Hidden damage, late selections and extra requests are the usual causes. A written scope and change orders keep it in check.

Should I get three bids?

Yes. Compare scope, not just price, and check that each bid includes permits and licensed trades.

How much should I keep as a contingency?

Many remodelers suggest 10 to 20 percent, more for older homes.

Are these your prices?

No. They are typical Houston ranges. Prices marked as ours come from our own price list.
