Bathroom Remodeling in Houston, TX

Bathroom remodeling in Houston is about water done right. We waterproof every shower, use licensed plumbers and electricians and hold to a fixed price and written dates, so you know when you get your bathroom back.

Signs it is time to remodel your bathroom

  • Cracked or stained tile
  • Soft spots near the tub or toilet
  • Grout and caulk that keep growing mildew
  • An old tub or shower that leaks
  • No exhaust fan or a fan that does not vent outside
  • Not enough storage or counter space
  • An outdated or cramped layout
  • You want a walk-in shower or a better step-in tub

Good to know in Houston

Houston bathrooms deal with heat, humidity and the occasional plumbing leak. Poor ventilation fuels mold, so a quiet exhaust fan that vents to the outside is a must. Slab homes make drain moves expensive, and older bungalows often hide rot under the tub. HomeAdvisor lists a Houston full bathroom remodel averaging around $11,900.

Plumbing and electrical work must be done by TSBPE and TDLR-licensed trades. Mold over 25 square feet needs a licensed mold remediator in Texas. Homestead remodels need a written contract signed by both spouses and the Texas Property Code 53.255 disclosure.
