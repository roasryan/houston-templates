Kitchen Remodeling in Houston, TX

Kitchen remodeling in Houston goes best when the price, the start date and the finish date are all in writing before demo. We build fixed-price scopes, use licensed plumbers and electricians, and hold a clear line on change orders.

Signs it is time to remodel your kitchen

  • The layout wastes space or blocks traffic
  • Cabinets are worn, sagging or hard to reach
  • Counters are chipped, stained or too small
  • Old wiring cannot handle modern appliances
  • Water damage under the sink or around the dishwasher
  • The kitchen is dark with few outlets
  • You want an open plan to the living room
  • You plan to sell and the kitchen hurts your listing

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s humidity makes cabinet materials and finishes matter: plywood boxes hold up better than particleboard near a dishwasher or sink. Many older homes inside the Loop hide rotted subfloor, galvanized supply lines and knob-and-tube wiring behind the cabinets. Public cost guides list a full Houston kitchen remodel averaging about $52,600.

Texas has no state remodeler license, but plumbing must be done by TSBPE-licensed plumbers and electrical by TDLR-licensed electricians. Homestead remodels need a written contract signed by both spouses and the disclosure statement under Texas Property Code 53.255. Homes built before 1978 need lead-safe work.

Questions about kitchen remodels

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most take 6 to 12 weeks after demo starts. Custom cabinets can add a few weeks of lead time before that.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Houston?

HomeAdvisor lists a Houston average near $52,600 for a full remodel. Your price depends on size, cabinets, counters and layout.

Do I need a permit?

Most kitchen remodels need a City permit, especially with plumbing, electrical or wall changes. We pull it.

Can I live in the house during the remodel?

Yes, but plan for a temporary kitchen and dust. We seal off the work area and clean daily.
