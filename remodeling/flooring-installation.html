Flooring Installation in Houston, TX

Flooring installation in Houston depends on moisture. Slab floors, humidity and the risk of water make some floors a better choice than others. We help you pick, fix the subfloor and install with dates in writing.

When to replace your flooring

  • Cracked or loose tile
  • Warped or cupped hardwood
  • Stains that never come out of carpet
  • Floors that squeak or feel soft
  • Water damage from a leak or flood
  • You want one floor through the whole house
  • Allergies or pets are hard on carpet
  • The existing floor is a mix of different surfaces

Good to know in Houston

Houston slab homes can wick moisture, and humid air makes solid wood cup. Waterproof vinyl and tile are common here for that reason. After a leak or a flood, wet subfloor must be dried and tested before new flooring goes on.

Flooring needs no state license, but work around plumbing or electrical needs licensed trades. Homes built before 1978 may have lead paint under baseboards, which requires lead-safe practices.

Questions about flooring

What floor is best for Houston humidity?

Luxury vinyl plank, porcelain tile and engineered wood handle it best. Solid hardwood can cup.

Can you install over my old floor?

Sometimes. We check the old floor’s condition and height.

How long does a flooring install take?

A single room can take a day. A whole house can take one to two weeks.

Do I need to move furniture?

We can move light furniture. Heavy items you should empty or tell us about before the visit.
