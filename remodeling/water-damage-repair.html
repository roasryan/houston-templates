Water Damage Repair in Houston, TX

Water damage repair in Houston has to move fast, because mold starts within a couple of days in our humidity. We assess the damage, dry the structure, replace rotted material and rebuild with a written scope.

Signs you have water damage

  • Ceiling stains or sagging drywall
  • Soft or spongy floors
  • Musty smell
  • Peeling paint or bubbling wallpaper
  • Warped baseboards or flooring
  • Visible mold or black spots
  • Water stains below a bathroom or washer
  • Rising water bills with no clear leak

How a water damage repair works

  1. 1Stop the waterWe help you find the source. A licensed plumber fixes the leak if needed.
  2. 2Assess and photographWe map wet areas with a meter and photograph everything for insurance.
  3. 3Dry out and removeWet drywall, insulation and flooring are removed. Fans and dehumidifiers dry the structure.
  4. 4RebuildWe replace subfloor, framing, drywall, paint and flooring.
  5. 5Close out with insuranceWe provide a written scope and photos to support your claim.

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s history with floods, from Allison to Harvey, shapes how we rebuild. Wet drywall and insulation must come out, and the structure must dry before anything is closed up. In a hot, humid climate, mold can start in 24 to 48 hours.

In Texas, mold remediation over 25 contiguous square feet must be done by a licensed mold remediation contractor under TDLR. Plumbing repairs need a TSBPE-licensed plumber, and structural work in a floodplain can trigger City of Houston rules.

Questions about repairs after water damage

Does insurance cover water damage?

Many policies cover sudden leaks but not slow ones or floods. Call your insurer first and take photos.

How fast does mold grow?

It can begin within a day or two in warm, damp conditions.

Do you handle the insurance paperwork?

We provide the scope, photos and invoices your adjuster needs.

Can you repair a floodplain home?

Yes. If damage is over half the home’s value, extra City rules may apply. We help you check.
