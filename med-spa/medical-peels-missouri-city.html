Medical-grade peels in Missouri City

Medical-grade peels in Missouri City use a solution that exfoliates the top layers of skin so fresh skin shows through. At Riverstone Aesthetics & Wellness, a peel is $150 and is chosen for your skin type. Results vary, and your provider explains the risks.

Good to know before a peel

  • A good-faith exam is done before certain peels
  • Tell us about acne medicine, retinol and past peels
  • You may feel stinging and see redness or flaking
  • Sun protection is a must after a peel
  • Do not pick or pull peeling skin
  • Avoid waxing or harsh products for a few days
  • Pregnancy and some medicines may mean a different plan

Good to know in Houston

Texas sun is the biggest factor for peeled skin. Use SPF 30 or higher every day, wear a hat, and plan a peel for a time when you can limit sun for about a week. Cooler months are often the easiest.

Medical peels are done under physician oversight in Texas. A physician, NP or PA reviews you, and the delegating physician’s name is posted in the lobby.

Questions about medical-grade peels

How much is a chemical peel in Houston?

How long does peeling last?

For a light peel, a few days. Deeper peels take longer.

Can I go out in the sun afterward?

Avoid it. Use SPF and a hat, and follow your provider’s instructions.
