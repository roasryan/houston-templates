A body composition scan in Houston shows a snapshot of fat, muscle and water so you can track real progress. At Bayou Strength & Conditioning, a fitness assessment with an InBody scan is $75. It gives you and your coach a starting point.

What the assessment is for

  • You want a baseline before you start
  • You want to track more than the scale
  • You want movement checks and a plan
  • You want to see how muscle and fat change over time
  • You want your coach to see how you move
  • You are a member and want a quarterly scan

Good to know in Houston

Body water levels shift in Houston heat, which can change scan results. Try to scan at the same time of day, fed and hydrated the same way, for useful comparisons.

A body scan is a fitness tool and is not a medical test. If you have health concerns, talk with your doctor.

Questions about body scans and assessments

How much is a body scan in Houston?

Usually about $20 to $150 depending on what is included. Ours is $75 for the full assessment.

Is an InBody scan accurate?

It gives a useful estimate. Results shift with water, food and time of day.

Is it safe?

Most people can use it. Ask first if you have a pacemaker or are pregnant.

How often should I scan?

Every 4 to 12 weeks is typical.
