Good to know in Houston

Houston traffic and heat shape gym habits. Many members come at 5 AM or after work and use the indoor floor to avoid heat. Hours that start early help.

Texas gyms must register under the Health Spa Act, give you a written contract and honor a 3-business-day right to cancel for new contracts.
