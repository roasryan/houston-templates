Good to know in Houston

Houston has great food and big portions, so coaching often focuses on how to eat out and still make progress. Heat and humidity also change how much water you need.

A coach is not a doctor or a registered dietitian. If you have a medical condition or an eating concern, ask your doctor or a dietitian first.
