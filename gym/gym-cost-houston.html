What a gym costs in Houston

Gym prices in Houston run from about $10 a month at big-box gyms to $250 or more at coached boutique studios. These are typical ranges, not quotes. Where we list our own prices, they belong to this demo gym.

What changes the price

  • Coaching included or not.
  • Class size and equipment.
  • Hours and location.
  • Contract length and fees.
  • Add-ons like body scans or nutrition.
  • Membership level.

Ways to save

  • Start with the free first session and ask what is included.
  • Choose classes or open gym, not both, until you know what you use.
  • Buy a PT pack if you will use it. Our 10-pack is $850.
  • Ask about the enrollment fee and any other fees up front.
  • Read the contract and the cancel terms before you sign.

Cost questions

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Texas law gives you a 3-business-day right to cancel a new contract, and the contract must spell out other cancel rights.

What if I miss a class?

Cancel classes at least 2 hours ahead. Late cancels and no-shows are $15.

Is the first session free?

Yes. Meet a coach, try a workout and leave with a plan, with nothing to sign that day.

Do coaches have certifications?

Texas has no state license for trainers, so we check for accredited certifications and a current CPR and AED card.
