AC Repair in Houston, TX

AC repair in Houston is mostly about speed. When it is 98° with 80% humidity, a house gets hot fast. Our techs find the problem, show you the price in writing, and fix most things on the first visit.

Signs your AC needs repair

  • Air from the vents feels warm or only a little cool
  • Ice on the copper line or the indoor coil
  • The outdoor fan or compressor will not start, or hums and stops
  • The system runs all day and the house still sits above the set temperature
  • Water on the floor, in the drain pan, or on the ceiling below the attic unit
  • A loud buzz, squeal or rattle that was not there last week
  • The breaker trips when the AC starts
  • Your electric bill jumped with no change in how you use the AC

What changes the price

  • Which part failed, and whether it is in stock
  • Age of the system and whether it still takes R-410A
  • Refrigerant leaks cost more because the leak has to be found and sealed first
  • Attic units that are hard to reach take longer than a ground-level unit
  • Summer rush: a same-day visit in July may cost more than a booked day in April

Good to know in Houston

Houston AC units run 8 to 10 months a year, so parts wear out faster than in cooler cities. High humidity makes drain lines grow algae, and a clogged line is one of the most common calls from June to September. Salt air near Galveston Bay and the Ship Channel also eats condenser coils on the east side.

Texas requires air conditioning work for hire to be done by a contractor licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). Handling refrigerant also needs EPA 608 certification.

Questions about ac repair

How fast can you come for AC repair in Houston?

In summer we try to book same day. You get a text with an arrival window and another when the tech is on the way. If we miss the window, the diagnostic fee is waived.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If the repair is under about a third of the cost of a new system and the unit is under 12 years old, repair usually makes sense. We show both prices side by side.

Why is my AC freezing up?

Low airflow from a dirty filter or a weak blower is the most common cause. Low refrigerant is the next. Turn the AC off and let the ice melt before the visit.

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

Ask when you book. We give the price in writing before work starts, so there is no surprise.
