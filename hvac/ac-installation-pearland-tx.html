AC installation in Pearland, TX

Pearland has grown fast since the 1990s, and much of the housing is 15 to 30 years old. Neighborhoods like Shadow Creek Ranch, Silverlake and Southdown now have original AC systems reaching the end of their life. That makes Pearland one of the busiest places for system changeouts.

Many two-story homes here have the air handler in the attic and the outdoor unit on a pad near the back fence. Two-story houses often need two systems, and the upstairs unit works hardest. We size each one using a load calculation, since an oversized unit will cool fast but leave the air damp.

Pearland sits on clay soil that shifts with wet and dry spells. Pads can tilt over time, and we level or replace them so the new condenser stays flat and quiet.

Pearland has its own permit process through the city. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection and handle the paperwork, so you see one price in writing and one point of contact from estimate to walkthrough. We also look at the attic space and the duct runs before we quote, because a good system on weak ducts will still leave rooms warm. If you are not sure whether to replace now or wait, we explain how many years your current unit likely has left.
