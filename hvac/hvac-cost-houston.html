What AC work costs in Houston

AC work in Houston costs more than in cooler cities because the system runs most of the year. These are typical ranges, not quotes. Your price depends on your house, your system and the part that failed.

What changes the price

  • System size in tons and efficiency level
  • Single-stage, two-stage or variable-speed
  • Attic, closet or ground-level install
  • Ductwork condition and any repairs
  • Permit and inspection fees
  • Season: summer rush pricing can be higher than spring or fall

Ways to save

  • Book a tune-up in spring so small problems get fixed before July.
  • Change the filter every 1 to 3 months.
  • Ask for good, better and best prices, and compare them in writing.
  • Join a maintenance plan if you plan to stay in the home for years.
  • Get a second opinion on any repair over $1,500 before you decide to replace.

Cost questions

Why does AC cost so much in Houston?

The system runs most of the year, parts wear out sooner, and the climate needs the right size and dehumidifying power. Permits and inspections add cost on changeouts.

Is it cheaper to repair or replace my AC?

Repair is cheaper up front. Replacement makes sense if the unit is old, uses R-22, or the repair is more than a third of the cost of a new one.

Do you offer financing?

Ask about financing when you get your estimate. We show cash prices in writing first.

Are these prices your prices?

No. They are typical Houston ranges from public sources. Prices marked as ours are from our own list.
