Car lockout and fuel help in Houston

Car lockout service in Houston gets you back into your car without damaging the door. We also deliver fuel if you run dry. A driver comes to you, and we quote the price before we start.

When to call for a lockout or fuel

  • Keys are locked inside the car
  • The key fob battery died
  • A kid or pet is locked in, which is an emergency, so call 911 first
  • You ran out of gas
  • The fuel gauge is on empty and the car stalls
  • A key broke in the lock
  • You wrongly fueled the car and need help
  • You lost your key
