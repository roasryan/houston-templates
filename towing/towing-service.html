Towing service in Houston, TX

Towing service in Houston should come with a price you hear before the truck hooks up. We quote the full price by phone or text, send a driver and photograph your car at pickup and drop-off. Our flatbed hook-up is $125 plus $5 a mile.

When to call for a tow

  • The car will not start and a jump did not help
  • Warning lights are on and the engine is smoking or overheating
  • You had a crash and the car is not safe to drive
  • The car lost power steering or brakes
  • A flat tire with no spare
  • The transmission will not shift
  • The car is stuck on the shoulder of the freeway
  • You need a car moved to a shop or home

Good to know in Houston

Houston tows often happen on busy freeways like I-45, I-10 and the Beltway, where traffic makes the shoulder risky. Summer heat causes overheats and dead batteries. Flooding after heavy rain can stall cars in underpasses, so call from a safe spot and stay out of the water.

Texas tow companies and drivers are licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR), and trucks need permits and insurance. Texas law also limits what a company can charge on certain non-consent tows.
