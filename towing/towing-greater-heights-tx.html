Towing in the Greater Heights, TX

Towing in the Greater Heights, TX covers a busy, older part of Houston north of downtown. The Heights has narrow streets, street parking and lots of restaurants. I-10, I-45 and Loop 610 are close, so breakdowns can happen fast.

Street parking means cars are often blocked or boxed in. A flatbed with the right ramps can load a low car without scraping. Street parking also means cars get towed for parking rule violations, and Texas has rules about signs and fees for private lots.

Older cars are common in the Heights, so dead batteries and overheating come up often. Heat and short trips are hard on batteries. If your car will not start, we can test and jump it, or tow you to a shop.

We serve the Greater Heights with flatbed tows, jump starts, lockouts and flat tire help. We quote the price before we hook up, and photograph your car at pickup and drop-off. Roadside Club helps if you park on the street and have a few lockouts a year.
