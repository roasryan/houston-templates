Flat tire help in Houston, TX

Flat tire help in Houston gets you off the shoulder and back on the road. A driver puts on your spare, or tows you to a tire shop if you do not have one. We quote the price before we start.

When to call for a flat tire

  • The tire is flat or the car feels like it is pulling
  • You hit a pothole or debris and heard a pop
  • The tire pressure light is on
  • The spare is flat or missing
  • You cannot loosen the lug nuts
  • The car is parked in an unsafe place
  • You have no jack or tools
  • The wheel is damaged

Good to know in Houston

Houston roads have construction debris, potholes and standing water that hides damage. Heat also raises tire pressure and can cause blowouts on worn tires. Flat tires on busy freeways are risky, so move to a safe place and call.

Texas has a Move Over law that requires drivers to slow down or move over for stopped emergency and tow vehicles. If you pull over, turn on your hazard lights and stay safe.

Questions about flat tire help

Can you fix the flat tire?

We swap on your spare. Patching is done at a tire shop, and we can tow you there.

What if I have no spare?

We tow you to a tire shop. Many newer cars have a sealant kit instead of a spare.

What are locking lug nuts?

Special nuts that need a key. If you do not have it, tell us when you call.

Is it safe to drive on a donut spare?

Only for short trips at low speed. Replace it with a full tire soon.
