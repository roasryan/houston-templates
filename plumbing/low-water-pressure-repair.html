Water Pressure and Hot Water Fixes

Low water pressure repair in Houston starts with a gauge test at your hose bib. Weak flow can come from a bad valve, a clogged line or a failing heater. Too much pressure can also harm pipes, so we check both.

Signs of a water pressure or hot water problem

  • Weak flow at every tap
  • Showers that run cold or lukewarm too soon
  • Banging or hammering pipes when a valve closes
  • Pressure that changes through the day
  • Leaky faucets or running toilets that keep returning
  • A hot water tank that rumbles
  • Pressure over 80 psi at a hose bib
  • Hot water takes a long time to arrive

How a pressure check works

  1. 1Gauge testWe connect a gauge to the hose bib to read static and running pressure.
  2. 2Check shutoffs and valvesThe plumber tests the main valve, meter valve and pressure reducing valve.
  3. 3Check the water heaterSediment, a bad dip tube or a failed element can kill hot water.
  4. 4Choose a fixOptions might be a new valve, expansion tank, line replacement or heater service.
  5. 5Test and setWe set pressure to a safe level and test every fixture.

Good to know in Houston

Some Houston neighborhoods have pressure over 80 psi from the city, which wears out heaters and fixtures fast. Older homes with galvanized lines lose flow as rust narrows the pipe. Garage and attic water heaters also lose heat on long pipe runs, so hot water takes longer to arrive.

Most pressure tests need no permit. Replacing pipe or a water heater can need a City of Houston permit and a TSBPE-licensed plumber.

Questions about water pressure and hot water

What is a normal water pressure?

Most homes run between 40 and 80 psi. Above 80 psi, fixtures and appliance hoses can wear out.

Why is my hot water pressure low but cold is fine?

The water heater may have sediment, or a hot line may be clogged. A plumber can test both.

What causes banging pipes?

Water hammer from fast-closing valves or loose pipes. An arrestor or a pressure fix often stops it.

Is low pressure a city issue?

Sometimes. We check your side first and tell you if it looks like a city main issue.
