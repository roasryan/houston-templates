Questions

How much does a water heater cost in Houston?

A 40 or 50 gallon tank water heater usually runs $1,400 to $2,400 installed. Tankless units run $3,000 to $5,500. You get the price in writing before we start.

Do you charge to come out?

A repair visit has a trip fee that goes toward the repair. VIP members never pay it. Estimates for bigger jobs like a new water heater or sewer line are free.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater?

Yes. The City of Houston requires a permit and an inspection, and it must be pulled by a licensed master plumber. We handle both.

What does a sewer line repair cost?

Spot repairs often run $2,500 to $6,000. Replacing a whole line under the slab can run $8,000 to $15,000 or more. We show camera video and give written options first.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Our plumbers are licensed through the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE). Apprentices always work under a licensed plumber.
