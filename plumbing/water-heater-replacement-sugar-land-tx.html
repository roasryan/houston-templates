Water heater replacement in Sugar Land

Sugar Land is a large suburb southwest of Houston with many homes built in the 1990s and 2000s. That means water heaters installed then are now 15 to 25 years old, well past their normal life. Replacements are one of the most common jobs here.

Master-planned communities like Sugar Creek, First Colony, Riverstone and Telfair have mostly two-story homes with the water heater in the attic or garage. Attic units need a drain pan and a drain line to prevent ceiling damage, and many older installs lack one.

We swap tank and tankless units, replace the expansion tank and shutoff valve, and set the pressure. Sugar Land has its own permit process, so we pull the right permit and arrange the inspection. You get a flat price in writing before work starts.

Hard use matters here too. Large homes with many bathrooms need the right size heater, or the second shower runs cold. We help you choose between a 50-gallon tank, a hybrid unit or a tankless system based on your family and your gas line.
