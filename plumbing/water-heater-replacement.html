Water Heater Replacement in Houston

Water heater replacement in Houston is something you want done fast and done right. We swap tank and tankless units, pull the City of Houston permit and haul the old one away, with the price in writing first.

Signs your water heater needs replacing

  • No hot water or only lukewarm water
  • The tank is 10 to 12 years old
  • Rust-colored water from hot taps
  • Popping or rumbling noises from the tank
  • Water pooling around the base
  • The pilot light keeps going out on a gas unit
  • Hot water runs out faster than it used to
  • A leak from the tank itself, not a fitting

Good to know in Houston

Many Houston water heaters sit in attics or garages, which makes access and venting a bigger part of the job. Garage units must sit on a platform in many cases because of fire code. After freezes or floods, ask us to check gas lines and water heater controls for damage before you relight.

The City of Houston requires a permit and inspection for a water heater replacement, and the permit is pulled by a City-registered master plumber. The minimum permit fee is $97.56.

Questions about water heaters

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Houston?

Yes. The City of Houston requires a plumbing permit and an inspection. We pull the permit and schedule the inspection.

How long does a swap take?

A standard tank swap takes about 2 to 4 hours. Tankless installs can take a full day.

Is tankless worth it?

It saves space and gives hot water on demand. The higher upfront cost takes years to pay back.

How long do water heaters last?

Tank units usually last 8 to 12 years. Tankless units can last 15 to 20 with yearly flushing.
