Leak Repair in Houston, TX

Leak repair in Houston starts with finding where the water goes. A small drip inside a wall or under a slab can run for weeks and cost thousands. Our plumbers locate it, give you a flat price in writing and fix it.

Signs you have a water leak

  • A water bill that jumps with no change in use
  • The meter keeps moving when every tap is off
  • Wet or warm spots on the floor, which can mean a slab leak
  • Musty smell, peeling paint or bubbling drywall
  • The sound of running water when nothing is on
  • Low water pressure in one part of the house
  • Mold or dark stains on baseboards and ceilings
  • Cracks in tile or a floor that feels soft

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and that movement can crack pipes under a slab. Homes built in the 1950s to 1970s often have cast iron drains and galvanized supply lines that fail in age. After a hard freeze like February 2021, hidden splits in attic pipes often show up weeks later as stains.

Texas requires plumbing work for hire to be done by a plumber licensed or registered with the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners (TSBPE). Repairs that replace pipe may need a City of Houston permit.

Questions about leak repair

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

Warm spots on the floor, the sound of running water and a high bill with no change in use are common signs. A plumber can confirm with a pressure test.

How can I check my meter for a leak?

Turn off every tap and appliance, then watch the leak indicator on the meter for a few minutes. If it moves, you have a leak.

Will insurance cover a leak?

Many policies cover sudden water damage but not the pipe itself. Call your insurer and take photos before repairs.

How fast can you stop a leak?

If water is flowing, shut the main valve first, then call us. We try to send a plumber the same day.
