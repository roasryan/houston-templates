Plumber in the East End, Houston

The East End is one of Houston’s oldest areas, with homes from the 1920s through the 1950s. Many are wood-frame bungalows on pier-and-beam foundations, and some are slab houses added later. That mix means a plumber here deals with galvanized supply lines, cast iron drains and a lot of past repairs.

Neighborhoods like Magnolia Park, Eastwood, Second Ward and Harrisburg have long, narrow lots and older sewer lines. Roots and shifting soil often crack the line between the house and the street. Pier-and-beam houses make leaks easier to reach, but they can hide wet crawlspaces.

We see a lot of low water pressure from old galvanized pipe, running toilets, leaking shutoffs and water heaters in tight closets or garages. If you are renovating, ask us about replacing galvanized supply with PEX or copper when walls are open.

Parking and gate access vary on narrow streets, so tell us when you book. Our plumbers text before they arrive. Water heater swaps get a City of Houston permit, and you receive a flat price in writing before we start.
