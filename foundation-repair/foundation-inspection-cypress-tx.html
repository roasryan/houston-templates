Foundation inspection in Cypress, TX

Free floor-level readings and a written report in 24 hours. If you don’t need repair, the report says so.

Cypress has thousands of newer homes in Towne Lake, Bridgeland, Coles Crossing and Fairfield. Newer does not mean no movement. Builders pour on clay, and the first long drought often shows a crack or a sticking door within the first ten years.

Many Cypress homeowners call us when a builder warranty is about to end. A floor-level reading now gives you a written baseline. If something changes later, you can see exactly when and where.

Our free inspection takes about an hour. We read every room, check the brick and the soil line, and look at gutters, sprinklers and trees near the slab. You get the report with photos within 24 hours.

If the readings are normal, we tell you so in writing and show you how to water and drain around the house to keep it that way. If repair makes sense, the plan and the price are in the report. No pressure.

Buying in Cypress? Ask us for readings during your option period, so you know before you close.
