Questions

How much does foundation repair cost in Houston?

Most repairs fall between $2,000 and $15,000, based on how many piers the house needs. The free check gives you an exact written price.

Is the foundation check really free?

Yes. Floor-level readings in every room, an outside check and a written report with photos within 24 hours. If you don’t need repair, the report says so.

Is my crack serious?

Hairline cracks are common. Cracks wider at one end, stair-step cracks in brick and doors that suddenly stick are worth a reading.

Is foundation repair licensed in Texas?

Texas does not issue a state license for foundation repair. Ask for an engineer review and a written, transferable warranty instead.

Can I stay home during the repair?

Yes. Most repairs take one to three days and you can stay in the house.
