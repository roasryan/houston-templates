Foundation repair in Pearland, TX

Free floor-level readings and a written report in 24 hours. If you don’t need repair, the report says so.

Pearland’s flat ground and clay soil hold water after heavy rain, then dry out quickly in summer heat. Homes in Silverlake, Shadow Creek Ranch and the older streets near Old Town Pearland all sit on that same moving soil.

Flat lots mean water often has nowhere to go. We see a lot of Pearland homes where the real problem is drainage: gutters that empty at the slab and beds that hold water against the brick. Fixing that early can keep a small problem small.

Our free check gives you floor-level readings in every room and a written report with photos within 24 hours. If the numbers are normal, the report says so.

If piers are needed, we show you how many, where they go and what it costs before anything is scheduled. Most Pearland repairs take one to three days, and you can stay in the house the whole time.

We also test the plumbing under the slab when readings point to one low area, so the cause is fixed before the house is lifted.
