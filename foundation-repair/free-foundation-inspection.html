Free Foundation Inspection in Houston, TX

Not sure if that crack matters? That is the most common question we get. Our free check measures your floor level room by room and gives you a written answer within 24 hours, even if the answer is that you don’t need us.

Signs you may need foundation inspections

  • A new crack that is wider at one end
  • Doors or windows that started sticking this year
  • A crack in the brick that you can fit a coin into
  • Floors that feel like they slope
  • You are buying or selling a home
  • Your home inspector flagged the foundation
  • A neighbor on your street just had repairs
  • You saw soil pulled away from the slab in summer

Good to know in Houston

Houston homes move a little every year as the clay swells and shrinks. A reading this year gives you a baseline, so next year you can see if anything changed.

Buying a home? Ask for the floor-level readings before your option period ends.

Questions about foundation inspections

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. The check and the written report are free. You only pay if you choose repair or ask for an engineer letter.

How long does it take?

About 45 to 60 minutes at the house. The written report comes within 24 hours.

Will you try to sell me piers?

No. If the readings are normal, the report says so. Our inspectors are not paid more for selling piers.

What is a floor-level reading?

A digital level measures the height of the floor in each room, so you see where the slab has dropped or lifted.
