Pier and Beam Foundation Repair in Houston, TX

Older Houston homes in neighborhoods like The Heights and Montrose sit on piers and beams over a crawl space. When piers settle or wood rots, floors sag and bounce. We crawl under, measure, and show you exactly what needs shimming, replacing or adding.

Signs you may need pier and beam repair

  • Floors that feel soft, bouncy or sag in the middle of a room
  • Gaps under baseboards or between the floor and the wall
  • Doors that drag on the floor or stick at the top
  • Musty smell or standing water in the crawl space
  • Rotted sill plates or beams you can see from outside
  • Piers leaning or blocks shifted off their pads
  • Cracks in plaster or drywall upstairs
  • Windows that won’t open after rain

Good to know in Houston

Many pre-1950s Houston homes sit on cedar or concrete block piers. Wet springs and dry summers move those piers more than you would expect, and floors start to roll.

Most pier and beam work happens from the crawl space, so the yard and landscaping usually stay as they are.

Questions about pier and beam repair

How often do pier and beam homes need re-shimming?

Every few years is common in Houston clay. A yearly floor check catches it while it is a small job.

Can you fix bouncy floors without tearing them up?

Usually yes. Most fixes are done from the crawl space.

Do you treat the crawl space too?

We look at drainage and moisture, because water under the house is often the cause.

Do I need to leave the house?

No. You can stay home during most pier and beam work.
