What changes the price

  • Number of piers, and whether any are inside the house
  • Pier type: pressed concrete, steel or helical
  • How much lift the house needs
  • Access around decks, patios and landscaping
  • Plumbing tests, engineer letters and permits where the city requires them

Honest ways to save

  • Fix drainage and watering first if the readings are only a little off
  • Get a baseline reading now so you only repair what moves
  • Ask for the pier map, not just a total price

Cost questions

Why does foundation repair cost so much in Houston?

Clay soil moves a lot, so piers have to reach soil that stays steady. The number of piers drives the price.

Is the cheapest bid the best?

Compare the number and type of piers, the warranty and whether an engineer reviews the plan. Fewer piers can mean the job gets redone.

Do you offer financing?

Financing is available. Check your rate at the inspection.

Does homeowners insurance pay?

Usually not for settling. A sudden plumbing leak may be covered. Ask your agent.
