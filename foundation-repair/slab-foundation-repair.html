Slab Foundation Repair in Houston, TX

Most Houston homes sit on a concrete slab poured on clay soil. When the clay dries out or soaks up water, the slab moves and the cracks show up inside and out. We measure first, then show you in writing whether it needs piers, and how many.

Signs you may need slab foundation repair

  • Stair-step cracks in brick, often starting at a window corner
  • Diagonal cracks in drywall above doors and windows
  • Doors that stick, swing open on their own or won’t latch
  • Gaps between the wall and the window frame or trim
  • Floors that slope or a ball that rolls to one side
  • Cracked floor tile in a line across the room
  • Soil pulled away from the slab edge in summer
  • Brick mortar falling out near the corners of the house

Good to know in Houston

Houston sits on expansive clay. It swells when it is wet and shrinks when it is dry, and a slab sitting on top moves with it. Long dry summers followed by heavy rain are when most new cracks show up.

Texas does not issue a state license for foundation repair. That makes the written report, an engineer review and a warranty you can transfer when you sell more important than any badge.

Questions about slab foundation repair

How do I know if my slab really needs repair?

A floor-level reading tells you. Some movement is normal. We measure every room and tell you in writing whether the numbers call for repair.

How long does slab repair take?

Most homes take 1 to 3 days. You can stay in the house while we work.

Will my homeowners insurance pay for it?

Most policies do not cover settling or soil movement. A sudden plumbing leak can be different, so check your policy and ask your agent.

Is the warranty transferable?

Yes. The pier warranty lasts for the life of the house and transfers to the next owner, which helps when you sell.
