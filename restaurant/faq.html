Restaurant questions, answered

General

Do you take reservations?

Yes, book online or call. Walk-ins are welcome, but weekends fill up fast, so we suggest a reservation.

How much does the private room cost?

Brunch starts at $42 a guest and dinner has a $1,500 food and drink minimum. A 20% service charge is added, and a 50% deposit holds your date.

How many people does the private room seat?

Up to 30 guests. We need your final count 72 hours before the party.

Can you handle food allergies?

Yes. Tell us when you book and the chef prepares a separate plate, with the allergy flagged on the ticket.

Do you cater?

Yes. Trays that serve 10 run $90 to $220 and are ready for pickup. Call a few days ahead for big orders.

What is the Table Club?

A $10 a month membership with reservations 30 days ahead, a free dessert on your birthday and first look at seasonal menus.

Is there parking?

Street parking in the Heights can be tight at busy times, so plan a few extra minutes.

Date night

Can I request a quiet table?

Yes. Add a note when you book and we do our best.

Should I book for a weeknight?

A reservation is not always needed, but it is safer, since the room can fill with groups.

Do you do anniversary desserts?

Tell us when you book. We can bring a candle with dessert.

Can we skip the wine?

Of course. Water, tea, coffee and non-alcohol drinks are on the menu.

Weekend brunch

What days is brunch served?

Saturdays and Sundays.

Can I book a big group?

Yes. Parties up to 30 can use our private room, and brunch there starts at $42 a guest.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Ask when you book. We can point you to meat-free plates.

Is the mimosa bar included?

It is an add-on for the private room at $320 for up to 20 guests.

Birthdays and celebrations

Can I bring my own cake?

Yes. We cut and plate it for free.

Can you make it a surprise?

Tell us the plan when you book and we help.

How many can you seat?

Tables seat small groups. The private room seats up to 30.

Do you do kids’ birthday dinners?

Yes, with family-style plates. Ask for a table that suits the age of your guests.

Private room

How much does the private room cost?

Brunch starts at $42 a guest and dinner has a $1,500 food and drink minimum. A 20% service charge is added, and a 50% deposit holds your date.

How many people does it seat?

Up to 30 guests. We need your final count 72 hours before the party.

Is the room private?

Yes. It is your own room for 2.5 hours with a dedicated server and bartender.

Can I decorate?

Yes, in a way that does not damage the room. Centerpieces are an add-on.

Catering trays

How far ahead should I order?

A few days for big orders. Call and we will tell you what is possible.

Do you deliver?

Pickup is standard. Ask if delivery is available for your order.

How many does a tray serve?

About 10 people.

How should I keep food safe?

Keep hot food hot and cold food cold, and eat or chill leftovers within two hours.
