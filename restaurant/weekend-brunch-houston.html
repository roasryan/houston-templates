Weekend brunch in Houston, TX

Weekend brunch in Houston is a long, slow meal with family and friends. Magnolia & Mesquite serves family-style plates, biscuits and a mimosa bar on Saturdays and Sundays. Book a table online.

Good to know before brunch

  • Brunch runs Saturdays and Sundays
  • Reservations are best for groups of four or more
  • The mimosa bar is available for groups
  • Family-style plates are made to share
  • Kids are welcome
  • Ask for a table inside if the patio is hot
  • Bring your appetite, since plates are large
  • Allergies are handled with a separate plate

Good to know in Houston

Houston weekend brunch crowds peak between 10 AM and 1 PM, so early or late tables are easier to get. Summer humidity makes patio seating warm by midday. Heights streets fill up with brunch traffic, so leave time to park.

Alcohol is served under TABC rules, and guests must be 21 or older to order. Ask about mimosa hours when you book.
