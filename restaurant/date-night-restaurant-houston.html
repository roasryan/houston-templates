Good to know in Houston

Heights date nights often mean a stroll down 19th Street or a walk on the Heights hike and bike trail first. Houston evenings can be hot, so plan for a short walk. Friday and Saturday are busy, so a mid-week table is quieter.

Alcohol is served under TABC rules. Guests must be 21 to order, and we may ask for ID.

Questions about date night

Can I request a quiet table?

Yes. Add a note when you book and we do our best.

Should I book for a weeknight?

A reservation is not always needed, but it is safer, since the room can fill with groups.

Do you do anniversary desserts?

Tell us when you book. We can bring a candle with dessert.

Can we skip the wine?

Of course. Water, tea, coffee and non-alcohol drinks are on the menu.
