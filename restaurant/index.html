Questions

Do you take reservations?

Yes, book online or call. Walk-ins are welcome, but weekends fill up fast, so we suggest a reservation.

How much does the private room cost?

Brunch starts at $42 a guest and dinner has a $1,500 food and drink minimum. A 20% service charge is added, and a 50% deposit holds your date.

How many people does the private room seat?

Up to 30 guests. We need your final count 72 hours before the party.

Can you handle food allergies?

Yes. Tell us when you book and the chef prepares a separate plate, with the allergy flagged on the ticket.

Do you cater?

Yes. Trays that serve 10 run $90 to $220 and are ready for pickup. Call a few days ahead for big orders.
