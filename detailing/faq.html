Car detailing questions, answered

General

How much does mobile detailing cost in Houston?

A maintenance wash starts around $79 and a full detail on a midsize car is about $260. A 2-step correction with a 5-year ceramic coating starts near $1,950.

Do you need my water and power?

No. We bring our own water and power. We just need a flat spot where the car can sit for a few hours.

How do you get rid of lovebug damage?

Lovebugs are acidic and can etch the clear coat if they sit in the Houston sun. Fresh splatter washes off, and older etching needs a polishing step.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Ours is a 5-year coating. It needs a gentle hand wash and a yearly inspection, about $199, to keep the warranty good.

Is mobile washing legal in Houston?

Yes, when wash water stays out of the storm drain. Houston can fine you for dumping wash water, so we use rinseless methods and a reclaim mat.

Our maintenance wash starts around $79 for a car. Trucks and large SUVs usually cost more.

Do I need to be home?

No, if the car is accessible and the spot is flat. We text you when we arrive and when we are done.

Do you need my water or power?

No. We bring our own water and power.

What if it rains?

We reschedule at no charge.

Full detail, inside and out

How long does a full detail take?

Usually 3 to 5 hours for a midsize car. Heavy pet hair or stains add time.

How much is a full detail in Houston?

Our midsize full detail is about $260. Trucks, SUVs and very dirty cars usually cost more.

How long does it last?

The spray sealant looks good for 6 to 8 weeks. A coating lasts longer.

Can you do two cars at once?

Yes. Ask when you book, since a second vehicle changes the time.

Interior detail

Can you remove old stains?

Many come out, but not all. We tell you what to expect before we start.

How long does an interior detail take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours. Heavy pet hair or stains add time.

Is the interior safe to use right after?

Seats and carpet are damp, so they need some dry time. Leave windows open.

Do you handle flood-damaged cars?

We clean them, but flood damage can hide in wiring and padding. Ask us what we can and cannot fix.

Paint correction

Will paint correction remove all scratches?

It removes swirls and light scratches. Scratches that catch a fingernail may be too deep to polish out.

How long does it take?

A 1-step polish takes about a day. A 2-step correction can take one to two days.

Does polishing remove paint?

It removes a very thin layer of clear coat. We measure thickness first to stay safe.

Do I need a coating after?

Not required, but a coating helps new paint stay glossy.

Ceramic coating

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Ours is a 5-year coating. It needs a gentle hand wash and a yearly inspection to keep the warranty.

Can I take it through an automatic wash?

We suggest hand washing. Brushes can scratch, even over a coating.

Does a coating stop scratches?

No. It adds hardness and slickness, but it does not make paint scratch-proof.

How much does ceramic coating cost in Houston?

Prices vary a lot by coating and prep. Ours starts near $1,950 with a 2-step correction.

Pet hair, odor and bug damage

How do you get rid of lovebug damage?

Fresh splatter washes off. Older etching needs a polishing step.

Can you remove all pet hair?

We remove most of it. Stubborn hair in carpet may take extra passes.

What is ozone treatment?

A machine fills the cabin with ozone to break down odor, then the car is aired out. It is not safe to sit in during treatment.

Will the smell come back?

If the source is gone, it should not. Wet carpet or a leak can bring it back.
