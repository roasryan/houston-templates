Questions

How much does mobile detailing cost in Houston?

A maintenance wash starts around $79 and a full detail on a midsize car is about $260. A 2-step correction with a 5-year ceramic coating starts near $1,950.

Do you need my water and power?

No. We bring our own water and power. We just need a flat spot where the car can sit for a few hours.

How do you get rid of lovebug damage?

Lovebugs are acidic and can etch the clear coat if they sit in the Houston sun. Fresh splatter washes off, and older etching needs a polishing step.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Ours is a 5-year coating. It needs a gentle hand wash and a yearly inspection, about $199, to keep the warranty good.

Is mobile washing legal in Houston?

Yes, when wash water stays out of the storm drain. Houston can fine you for dumping wash water, so we use rinseless methods and a reclaim mat.
