What changes the price

  • Vehicle size.
  • Starting condition of the paint and interior.
  • Number of polishing steps.
  • Coating grade and warranty.
  • Mobile versus shop service.
  • Add-ons like engine bay, wheels or odor treatment.

Ways to save

  • Pick a monthly plan if you wash often.
  • Do a maintenance wash before the dirt builds up.
  • Skip the coating if you will not keep the car.
  • Bundle add-ons into one visit.
  • Ask for photos and a written price first.

Cost questions

How much does mobile detailing cost in Houston?

A maintenance wash starts around $79 and a full detail on a midsize car is about $260. A 2-step correction with a 5-year ceramic coating starts near $1,950.

Is mobile detailing more expensive than a shop?

Often it costs about the same. You pay for the convenience, not a higher rate.

Is ceramic coating worth it?

If you keep the car and park outside, it can save time washing. It is not scratch-proof.

Do you charge extra for dirty cars?

Heavy pet hair, mud or stains add time, so those carry add-on fees. We tell you before we start.
