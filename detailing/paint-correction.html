Paint correction in Houston, TX

Paint correction in Houston removes swirls and light scratches so the paint shines. A machine polisher levels the clear coat, and we show you photos along the way. Showroom detail with a 1-step polish is $549 with us.

Signs your paint needs correction

  • Fine circular swirls in sunlight
  • Dull or hazy paint
  • Light scratches that stay after washing
  • Water spots that will not come off
  • Etching from bugs or bird droppings
  • Faded or chalky paint
  • You plan to add a ceramic coating
  • Automatic washes left marks

How paint correction works

  1. 1Inspect the paintWe check with a light and measure paint thickness.
  2. 2Wash and clay barThe car is washed and decontaminated so we do not grind dirt in.
  3. 3Test spotWe try a small area to pick the right pad and compound.
  4. 4PolishA machine polisher removes defects, usually in one or two steps.
  5. 5Wipe and protectWe check with a light and apply a sealant, or a coating if you chose one.

Good to know in Houston

Houston sun, bugs and hard water all damage clear coat. Lovebugs are acidic and can etch paint when left in the heat. Sprinklers leave mineral spots, and cars parked outside get faded faster.

Questions about paint correction

Will paint correction remove all scratches?

It removes swirls and light scratches. Scratches that catch a fingernail may be too deep to polish out.

How long does it take?

A 1-step polish takes about a day. A 2-step correction can take one to two days.

Does polishing remove paint?

It removes a very thin layer of clear coat. We measure thickness first to stay safe.

Do I need a coating after?

Not required, but a coating helps new paint stay glossy.
