Interior car detailing in Houston

Interior car detailing in Houston gets the dirt out of seats, carpet, vents and cup holders. We vacuum, steam and shampoo, then dry and protect the surfaces. It is the fix for spills, crumbs and kid messes.

Signs your interior needs a detail

  • Stains on seats or carpet
  • Crumbs deep in seat cracks
  • A sour or musty smell
  • Dust on the vents and dash
  • Sticky cup holders or door pockets
  • Kids’ messes or food spills
  • Sweaty or smoky odor
  • Pet hair stuck in the fabric

How an interior detail works

  1. 1Remove trash and floor matsWe clear the car and check for stains.
  2. 2Vacuum everythingTight spots get brushes and compressed air.
  3. 3Steam and shampooSteam loosens grime. We shampoo carpet and cloth seats or clean leather.
  4. 4Plastics, vents and glassWe wipe the dash, vents, buttons and inside glass.
  5. 5Protect and finishLeather gets conditioner and plastics get UV protection.

Good to know in Houston

Houston humidity can grow mildew in a car that gets damp, especially after floods or leaks. Heat bakes spilled drinks into carpet and makes smells worse. A deep clean plus a dry-out helps keep the smell gone.

Questions about interior detail

Can you remove old stains?

Many come out, but not all. We tell you what to expect before we start.

How long does an interior detail take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours. Heavy pet hair or stains add time.

Is the interior safe to use right after?

Seats and carpet are damp, so they need some dry time. Leave windows open.

Do you handle flood-damaged cars?

We clean them, but flood damage can hide in wiring and padding. Ask us what we can and cannot fix.
