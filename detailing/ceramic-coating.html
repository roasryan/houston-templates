Ceramic coating in Houston, TX

Ceramic coating in Houston adds a hard, slick layer over your paint that sheds dirt and is easier to wash. Ours is a 5-year coating installed after a 2-step correction, starting near $1,950. A yearly inspection, about $199, keeps the warranty good.

Is a ceramic coating right for you

  • You keep the car for several years
  • You park outside in the sun
  • You want paint that washes faster
  • You like a deep gloss
  • You want less water spotting
  • The paint is new or just corrected
  • You do not mind hand washing
  • You want protection from lovebug etching

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s sun, hard water and lovebugs are tough on paint. A coating makes bugs and water spots easier to remove. It does not stop rock chips or scratches, so it is not a replacement for paint protection film.

Coating warranties usually need a yearly inspection by a certified installer, and you should keep your install paperwork. No state license is needed for coating work.

Questions about ceramic coating

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Ours is a 5-year coating. It needs a gentle hand wash and a yearly inspection to keep the warranty.

Can I take it through an automatic wash?

We suggest hand washing. Brushes can scratch, even over a coating.

Does a coating stop scratches?

No. It adds hardness and slickness, but it does not make paint scratch-proof.
