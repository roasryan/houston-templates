Mobile car wash in Houston, TX

A mobile car wash in Houston means a careful hand wash at your driveway or office, with no drive to a wash bay. Our maintenance wash starts around $79 and keeps clean paint clean. We text when we are on the way and when we are done.

Who a maintenance wash is for

  • Your car is newer and you want to keep the paint nice
  • You park outside and get pollen, dust and bird droppings
  • You have a ceramic coating that needs a gentle wash
  • You do not have time to sit at a wash bay
  • You want a regular monthly clean
  • Your car is mostly clean and just needs a refresh
  • Automatic washes leave swirls or water spots
  • You want wheels, tires and glass done well

How a mobile wash works

  1. 1Book and confirmPick a time and send a photo or the car type. We confirm by text.
  2. 2We arrive with water and powerThe van is self-contained. You only need a flat spot.
  3. 3Hand washWe pre-rinse, wash with clean mitts and dry with soft towels. We use rinseless methods so water stays out of the storm drain.
  4. 4Wheels, tires and glassWheels are cleaned, tires dressed and glass done streak-free.
  5. 5Spray sealant and photosWe add a spray sealant, check the finish and text you photos.

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s pollen, lovebugs and summer sun mean a car can look dirty a week after a wash. Rain brings dirt and water spots, so a sealant helps water run off. Parking under trees also drops sap.

Wash water cannot go down a storm drain in Houston. We use rinseless methods and a reclaim mat, and the city can fine illegal wash water.
