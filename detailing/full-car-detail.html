Full car detail in Houston, TX

A full car detail in Houston cleans the inside and the outside in one visit at your home or office. A midsize car is about $260. You get a text when we arrive and before and after photos when we finish.

When a full detail makes sense

  • The car has not had a deep clean in a year or more
  • You are selling or trading in the car
  • Spills, crumbs and dust fill the interior
  • The paint feels rough after washing
  • You just bought a used car
  • Houston summer left the dash and seats dull
  • You want the car ready for a trip or family visit
  • The car still looks dirty after a normal wash

How a full detail works

  1. 1Walk-around and photosWe photograph the car and note scratches before starting.
  2. 2Interior deep cleanVacuum, steam and shampoo the carpet and seats. Plastics and vents are cleaned.
  3. 3Wash and clay barA hand wash, then a clay bar lifts bonded dirt from the paint.
  4. 4Wax or sealantWe protect the paint so it lasts 6 to 8 weeks.
  5. 5Final check and photosYou get after photos and a condition report.

Good to know in Houston

Houston’s sun fades dashes and dries out leather, and humidity can leave a damp smell. Lovebugs, pollen and construction dust stick to paint in spring and fall. A full detail resets the car before the next hard season.

No Texas license is needed for auto detailing, but a mobile business needs insurance and must keep wash water out of storm drains. We carry commercial auto and garagekeepers coverage.
