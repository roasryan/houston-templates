Weekly pool service in Houston

Weekly pool service in Houston keeps your water clear all season. The same tech visits on the same day, skims, brushes, vacuums, empties baskets and balances the water. You get readings after every visit.

Who weekly service is for

  • You do not want to test and balance the water yourself
  • You want your pool ready every weekend
  • Your pool turned green last summer
  • You travel for work
  • You have a heater, salt system or spa that needs care
  • Your HOA requires a clean pool
  • You are tired of buying chemicals

  2. 2CleanSkim, brush, vacuum and empty skimmer and pump baskets.

Good to know in Houston

Houston pools run almost all year. Heat, rain and sunlight eat chlorine quickly in summer, and storms dump leaves and debris. Algae can bloom in days if the water is out of balance, which is why weekly service pays off.

Never drain or backwash pool water into the street or a storm drain. Water goes to the sanitary sewer with permission, or onto the lawn after it is dechlorinated.
