What pool service costs in Houston

Pool service cost in Houston usually runs about $150 to $300 a month for weekly full service. Repairs and cleanups cost extra. Here are typical prices and what changes them.

Ranges are for planning only. Your written price comes after we see the job.

What changes the price

  • Pool size and type.
  • Trees and debris.
  • Chemicals included or not.
  • Equipment age.
  • Visit frequency.
  • Whether repairs need a licensed installer.

Ways to save

  • Pick a weekly plan instead of paying for repeated green-to-cleans.
  • Cover or trim trees to cut leaf load.
  • Clean baskets between visits.
  • Fix small leaks early before they become big ones.
  • Consider a variable-speed pump to cut power bills.

Cost questions

Why do prices vary so much?

Pool size, condition and what is included change the work. Written quotes help you compare.

Is a service contract worth it?

Weekly service often costs less than emergency green-to-cleans and chemical guesswork.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Quotes for service plans are free.

Does pool service include repairs?

Usually no. Repairs are quoted separately.
