Pool salt system and leak repair in Houston

Pool salt system and leak repair in Houston covers two common headaches. We clean and test salt cells, and we find leaks with pressure and dye tests. You get a written quote before repairs.

Signs of salt system or leak trouble

  • Low chlorine reading even with the salt cell running
  • Salt cell scale or white buildup
  • Error lights on the salt control
  • Pool loses more than an inch a week
  • Wet spots near the equipment pad
  • Cracks or soft ground near the pool
  • Higher water bills

How a salt or leak visit works

Good to know in Houston

Houston clay soil shifts with wet and dry swings, and that can crack pool plumbing and shells. Pool leaks often show up after a dry summer. Salt cells scale in hard water and wear out faster with heavy summer use.

Replacing or installing salt systems needs a Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) Residential Appliance Installer license. Never drain or backwash pool water into the street or a storm drain. Water goes to the sanitary sewer with permission, or onto the lawn after it is dechlorinated.

Questions about salt systems and leaks

How do I know if my pool is leaking?

Mark the water level and check after a week with the pump off. Losing more than evaporation is a sign.

Can you clean a salt cell?

Yes. Many cells last longer with regular cleaning.

Are salt pools low maintenance?

They need less chlorine handling, but cells and balance still need care.

Do you repair concrete cracks?

We locate the cause and refer major repairs to a pool builder.
