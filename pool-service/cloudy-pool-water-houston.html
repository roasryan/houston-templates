Cloudy pool water fix in Houston

Cloudy pool water in Houston usually comes from poor filtration, low chlorine or unbalanced water. We test the water, check the filter and pump and fix the cause. Then we clear the haze.

Common causes of cloudy water

  • Low chlorine or high pH
  • Dirty or clogged filter
  • Pump not running long enough
  • High calcium or stabilizer
  • Heavy rain or a pool party
  • Algae starting to bloom
  • Poor circulation from jets

How we clear cloudy water

  1. 1TestWe measure chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium and stabilizer.
  2. 2Check equipmentWe check the pump, filter pressure and baskets.
  3. 3TreatWe balance chemicals and add a clarifier if needed.
  4. 4Follow upWe re-test and run the pump until the water clears.

Good to know in Houston

Houston storms add dirt and organic matter to pools, and high heat uses up chlorine fast. Cloudy water after a big rain is common. Running the pump long enough each day, often 8 to 12 hours in summer, helps filtration.

Never drain or backwash pool water into the street or a storm drain. Water goes to the sanitary sewer with permission, or onto the lawn after it is dechlorinated.

Questions about cloudy water fixes

Is cloudy water safe to swim in?

Not until it clears. Cloudy water can hide poor sanitizer levels.

Can I fix it myself?

Often you can balance water and clean the filter. Call us if it stays cloudy.

How long does it take to clear?

Usually one to three days after the cause is fixed.

Could my pump be the problem?

Yes. A weak pump or dirty filter is a frequent cause.
